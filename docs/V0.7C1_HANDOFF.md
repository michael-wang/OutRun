## Engine sound

The original sawtooth/triangle engine through a 1200 Hz low-pass was changed to a triangle body plus sine undertone through a 600 Hz low-pass, with RPM-driven pitch, load-sensitive shift envelopes, softer wind, and lower tire tone. The existing gesture unlock, mute and pause behavior remains. Recordings: `baseline-engine.webm`, `engine-new.webm`, and the combined `desktop-drive-with-audio.webm`. Decoding to mono showed peak sample 0.173 (old first nine seconds) and 0.177 (new full run), with zero samples at or above 0.999 in either. Both original stereo channels of the new recording peaked at 0.125 with zero clipped frames. In the new full recording the largest adjacent-sample change was 0.0044 (zero changes above 0.2), a basic digital discontinuity check. Over the first nine seconds, decoded RMS was 0.0588 (old) and 0.0754 (new), and a first-order 1 kHz high-pass energy proxy fell from 0.167 to 0.013. The recordings accelerate at different rates, so these are descriptive signal measurements, not a controlled loudness or subjective comfort verdict. `audio-analysis.json` documents the calculation. Final sound comfort, actual speaker output, Android device behavior and vibration remain **UNVERIFIED** until human testing.
