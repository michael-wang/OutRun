# v0.4.2 — Gameplay-First Driving Prototype

**Decision:** The owner wants a REAL playable validation of two linked changes:
1. **Arcade Lateral Balance:** steering controls correction against a curve's outward drift, not a simulated wheel angle with persistent yaw/tire inertia.
2. **Gameplay-First Track Authoring:** specify *the amount of steering required at a chosen speed* before deriving curvature and generating the actual 3D roadway.

The earlier unexecuted v0.4.2 plan to tune yaw/slip and add arbitrary bends is superseded. Implement this prototype **in the existing local v0.4.1 game**, not by starting over.

## 1. Acceptance-oriented prototype, not a full editor

At minimum the owner must be able to repeatedly play, on PC and Pixel 8:
- **Low steering demand** corner;
- **Medium steering demand** corner;
- **High steering demand** corner;
- **Too-fast corner:** at the reference high speed, even full-lock steering cannot completely cancel outward drift, so the player must release GAS and/or use BRAKE to stay on asphalt;
- **One extra:** a readable **left-to-right or right-to-left S-curve** requiring sequentially changing correction, but not relying on stale inertial overshoot.

One contiguous course (roughly 60–90s at normal fast driving, loopable) can contain these sections plus brief acceleration/relief transitions. Preserve sunny 3D coast and basic scenery. Add clear **optional developer-only section markers** or `?challenge=...` scene start positions for reproducible testing; do not clutter normal play. Do not build an editor, designer dashboard, obstacle AI or dynamic difficulty system.

## 2. Quantized virtual steering: less fiddly, still visually analog

### Single source of truth

Raw control-position request `u_raw` stays in [-1,+1]. The car receives an **effective** quantized level `u_eff` from:
`{-1, -0.75, -0.5, -0.25, 0, +0.25, +0.5, +0.75, +1}`.

- **Default:** 4 distinct active steps per side + a true neutral. Store step count `N=4` as a simple configurable constant; structure conversion so it can later be tested at 3 or 5 without rewriting gameplay. Don't expose a complicated player Settings page for N yet.
- Touch strip remains horizontal, absolute-position-based. Finger touching/dragging at an x maps to raw position; quantize into the nearest step; release or cancellation immediately returns to neutral. Show finger-position marker smoothly (continuous-looking) but effective steering indicator can highlight a level. The display must **not** falsely show full analog authority.
- Use a generous intentional center dead zone (~6–10% of full width to tune) and small Schmitt-style hysteresis around band thresholds so small fingertip errors don't make the car rapidly toggle between adjacent levels. Ensure a deliberate swipe immediately changes level; avoid sluggish smoothing.
- On PC, A/D moves the **same virtual steering position** continuously while held; gameplay uses `u_eff` quantized from it. On release it springs swiftly back toward 0. Space = held GAS; Enter = held BRAKE. No browser scroll/focus hijack during active racing. Make opposite-key arbitration deterministic.
- Preserve mobile left steering strip + right stacked GAS/BRAKE and handedness swap, proper independent touch pointer IDs, long-press protection, fullscreen/PWA, safe areas.
- **No snap of the VEHICLE back to road center.** Steering control returning neutral changes only correction input, not car lateral position.

### Avoid quantization becoming another source of frustration

Since discrete levels cannot cancel arbitrary continuous bend demands exactly, **calibrate authored bend demands to land near the available effective levels** at a reference speed. Don't ask players to hold a continuously impossible 0.37 level if the only choices are 0.25 or 0.50. Road width / short transient tolerance should absorb rounding errors; no hidden recentering.

For human tuning, produce a simple log/readout of `rawSteer`, `effectiveSteer`, `requiredSteer` and `roadRelativeX`, and ideally record whether a level is matching, weaker or stronger than curve pressure. Disable debug labels in normal play.

## 3. Core driving: Arcade Lateral Balance, not tire simulation

Use road-relative coordinates: `s` = longitudinal distance along track; `x` = lateral offset from centerline. The actual 3D vehicle transform comes from track geometry `track(s)` + a sideways offset. The scene and camera remain 3D; do **not** replace rendering with sprite-only pseudo-3D.

At each fixed/reliable dt:
- `s` advances according to actual effective arcade speed (already approved **1.75×** world pace), throttle/brake, off-road slowdown; coordinate progression and visible geometry must agree.
- Local drift/correction velocity comes primarily from two opposing effects:
  `dx/dt = steeringAuthority(v) * u_eff - curvePressure(curvature(s), v)`.
  Set signs coherently: road turning right should push a non-steering car toward the outside/left.
- `steeringAuthority(v)` must be 0 or negligible when stationary and tuned for useful control at speed; `curvePressure` increases with curve severity and speed (consider a calibrated ~v² term). No large persistent `lateralVelocity` or `yawRate` integrator that makes x keep drifting after the bend ends.
- When `curvature=0` and `u_eff=0`, `dx/dt≈0`: preserve the existing lateral position, not automatic recentering. On a real bend, `u_eff=0` leads to outward movement and potentially off-road penalty. This difference is why this model does not repeat v0.1's autopilot bug.
- When curve demand exceeds full steering authority at a high speed, player can reduce speed by coasting/braking until full steering is enough. This is the game's skill challenge. Off-road slowdown is still only the penalty after failing.
- Maintain **bounded off-road corridor** so the car cannot disappear into landscape, keep forward progression at the outer limit and require **inward player steering** for reentry; do not spin at the limit.
- For rendering only, synthesize exciting car body yaw/roll, skid visuals and squeal from meaningful slip/imbalance/steering effort, with prompt falloff at curve exit. This visual rotation must not create uncommanded path inertia or make mild corrections shriek.
- Avoid calling the "centrifugal" effect physically accurate: this is designed **outward curve pressure** with an arcadey response equation.
- Treat the 1.75× WORLD PACE as an independent optical-flow/travel factor. Do not accidentally multiply curve force by `1.75²` unless explicit calibration proves that is wanted. Base difficulty on displayed reference speed and actual validated dynamics.

This pseudocode is directional, not an instruction to preserve old architecture. Inspect v0.4.1 movement code, implement the smallest possible replacement of its navigation update and adapt camera/drift rendering safely.

## 4. Difficulty-first inverse track specification (mandatory)

Define a **small typed challenge segment list** in project source with properties representing *desired gameplay*, NOT just spline control points:

- `id`, `label`, `turnDirection` (left/right/S/none);
- `referenceIndicatedSpeedKmH` (initial benchmark **230 km/h**, or document justified equivalent);
- `targetRequiredSteering` / signed normalized fraction;
- `nominalDurationAtReferenceSpeed` to size the segment in world/track distance, NOT trigger by game timer;
- `roadWidth` / tolerance margin;
- `elevationProfile` (simple rolling grades okay);
- `transitionLength` or gradual challenge ramp to avoid abrupt steps.

**Required prototype calibration points at about 230 indicated km/h:**

| Segment | Target magnitude of required steer | Player's intended action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low | ~0.25 | One steering notch balances the bend |
| Medium | ~0.50 | Two notches balance it |
| High | ~0.75 | Three notches balance it; less gives outward drift |
| Too fast | **~1.15–1.25** at 230 | Even `±1.0` is insufficient; slowing to a documented safe speed makes it manageable |
| S-bend | e.g. `-0.50` → `+0.50` | Player changes correction sign, center briefly, with no residual lateral drift once straight |

The values are *starting targets* and should be adjusted if rendered/played challenge is not legible. Use **BOTH left and right directions** across the course (the above list need not force a particular direction).

**Inverse construction requirement:** For a segment target and reference speed, derive the needed `curvePressure` and invert the chosen bend/curvature function to create road curvature. Integrate curvature to produce tangent heading and 3D centerline, then sweep a drivable road mesh with consistent road edges, heights, markers and off-road classification. For an S-bend use a smooth signed-curvature transition, not a sharp instantaneous geometry kink.

Do **not** first model five arbitrary curves, then retrospectively assign difficulty labels. A correctly designed small list is better than an elaborate procedural editor.

### Tolerances and fairness

- At the declared reference speed, a matching quantized level near 0.25/0.5/0.75 should hold lateral offset roughly steady over the core of its own segment, within a documented tolerance and enough asphalt margin for human fingers.
- With neutral steering, the same bend must create clear outward drift/off-road at speed (not necessarily instant death).
- At the 1.15–1.25 "too fast" segment, full steering at ~230 must produce net outward drift sufficient to require earlier braking/slowdown to remain on-road over the designed segment; demonstrate a successfully completed braked run at a lower speed. Don't use an impossible instant sharp bend or require unrealistically precise frame-perfect taps.
- Use lead-ins with visual warning/readability and gradual transitions. If quantization increases micro-oscillation, tune the dead zone/hysteresis and segment tolerance, not a magic always-on lane magnet.
- Once `requiredSteer=0` (straight), releasing steering makes `dx/dt≈0` promptly. Vehicle stays wherever in the lane/road shoulder the player left it.

## 5. Preserve scene and capabilities / non-goals

- Render actual 3D car and roadway with One continuous visible route, sensible coastal hills, readable curve look-ahead and no missing road/mountain occlusion. Verify road across loop seams.
- Don't reinvent camera/GUI/audio. Preserve accepted camera framing, fixed FOV/dolly, **car-left analog needle speedometer**, 1.75× visual pace, PC A/D+Space/Enter, Pixel 8 fullscreen/PWA/multitouch/swap/long press, off-road bounds/slowdown and menu.
- No traffic, rivals, obstacles beyond existing harmless roadside props, alternate cars, difficulty director, auto-DDA, route forks, convertible/passenger animation, music, scoring, or multiple stages in v0.4.2. Those belong to later milestones.
- Make the course replayable and practical to test on actual Pixel 8 and desktop, without complicated setup.

## 6. Definition of playable delivery

Build a *real* ~60–90s course driven with the new handling, containing the four demanded bend types and S-bend; include quick replay/start of specific sections via a dev-only flag if useful. Supply actual rendered-browser evidence for each segment, appropriate touch and keyboard input, tests and clear handoff.
