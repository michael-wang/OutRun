
PC: <http://localhost:5173/?lab=pilots>. Pixel on the same LAN: <http://192.168.1.105:5173/?lab=pilots> (verify the computer's address if it changes). The server was checked on the host; a physical phone connection remains unverified.

Keyboard while driving: `G` praise or `I` issue, then arrows or 1–4 choose praise, Enter saves and resumes, Escape cancels, and `[` / `]` select older/newer samples. Standard PS4 mapping: L1 or R1 opens praise; D-pad up/right/down/left selects the four printed tags; Cross saves/resumes, Circle cancels, Triangle cycles time and Square saves an issue flag. Gameplay triggers must return to neutral after the dialog before they can drive again. Buttons on PC menus show their actual shortcuts; touch menus suppress PC-only hints.

Praise defaults to a real sample about 2 seconds before the reaction. Earlier/Later controls select the nearest recorded Now/2/4/6/8/12-second option and display the actual offset and road position. The app keeps up to 15 seconds at roughly 10 Hz in memory, freezes it with the simulation, clears it on replay and preserves it after completion. Each event exports `anchor`/`eventAt`, `reactionAt`, the actual `offsetSeconds` and input origin. Pilot/L2, free drive and original brick examples have local-only feedback; the latter two have separate exports. New events and brick verdicts are stored separately from v0.7D.1 feedback; historical stores and archived reviews are not modified. The brick lab can export the prior v0.7D.1 verdicts separately. Existing reviews are included when the current pilot/L2 feedback is exported.

## Evidence and acceptance

- **AUDIO-01/03 PASS for browser signals; listening UNVERIFIED:** `audio-scenarios.json` and `audio-*.webm` cover left/right 60, 100, 150, 230 and 300 km/h starts and a gentle case. Both 60/100 cases stayed silent; loaded 230/300 turns produced sound on both sides. At 150 the tested left hairpin was audible while the less-loaded right approach was quiet; the mirrored equal-intensity unit test is symmetric. The old trigger treated road-relative movement as skid and could hold its release hysteresis while slowing. The new trigger requires speed, sustained curve load and steering effort, without changing `handling.ts`.
- **AUDIO-02 PASS for measured buses; subjective comfort UNVERIFIED:** `audio-measurements.json` contains separately recorded engine, tire and mixed buses. In loaded 230/300 cases tire RMS was approximately 15.6–15.7 dB below engine; mixed peaks were at most 0.195 in these captures. `audio-before-after.json` compares archived v0.7D.1 mixed recordings with matched v0.7D.2 reference starts. Tire oscillator was lowered to 510–760 Hz, modulation reduced and gain capped at 0.045. The warm engine graph is unchanged. Volume, timbre and no-clipping results are measurements, not a claim of human listening.
- **HINT-01/02 PASS:** `menu-1280.png` shows shortcuts directly on buttons; existing keyboard tests exercise those actions, focus and text/IME isolation. Touch layouts hide PC-only shortcut prefixes.
