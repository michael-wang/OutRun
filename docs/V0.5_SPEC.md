# v0.5 — High-Speed Spectacle

**Status:** Approved product direction; implementation has NOT started through this documentation task.
**Base:** Existing playable LOCAL v0.4.3 (implementation `84b18b3`, handoff `d370ef6`; inspect current HEAD).
**Human verdict:** FIRST version to deliver genuine pleasure in high-speed cornering. **Do not destabilize core driving.**

## User requests (25 Oct 2026)

1. The vehicle currently starts visibly yawing too late into a corner; lower the *visual* onset threshold.
2. The biggest car drift pose can be more dramatic than v0.4.3's reported 35–42°.
3. Roads that can be taken at maximum speed should be visually more spectacular / curved.

This milestone also opens the door to the previously deferred **original convertible**, but only after the core gameplay improvements remain successful.

## P0-A: More expressive but proportional body attitude

### Principle

The authoritative road-relative `s/x` position comes from **Arcade Lateral Balance**, curvature pressure, steering correction and speed. The rendered 3D **body yaw/lean** is a separate presentation layer. No yaw-rate inertia may spill into the next straight; no position is auto-centered and no bend is driven for the player.

### New shape / tuning targets

Do not merely linearly scale car angle from the quantized control input. Use a **smooth nonlinear envelope** combining applied steering, meaningful current road curvature/turn load, imbalance/slip state and speed, with quick release; values below are **visual calibration candidates**, not rigid global outputs:

| Situation | Target body pose / audio |
| --- | --- |
| Near-straight tiny correction | 0–3° yaw, quiet, no artificial skid |
| Real gentle bend, around 0.25 corrective steer | **3–8°** of readable subtle body response, normally no screech |
| Moderate curved section, about 0.50 | Roughly **12–22°** as bend load warrants; light lean |
| Hard, deliberately attacked bend, around 0.75 | Roughly **28–45°** dynamic car attitude with meaningful skid feedback |
| Very hard / intentional full-lock high-speed drift | Investigate **50–60° peak yaw**, optionally momentary **up to ~65°** if car, road and camera remain readable; make these brief memorable moments, not constant lock-on |

- **Earlier visual onset** on *real bends* is the actual goal. Avoid constantly tilting during small steering corrections on a straight. In particular a sustained 0.25 on a mild bend should show a subtle cue whereas v0.4.3 often looked motionless.
- Keep visually smooth attitude changes through soft steering detents, dynamic curve load and S-turn direction reversals. Apply quick, controlled visual easing and smooth sign transitions. A mild input must not trigger repeating tire shrieks or smoke.
- The rendered car pivots around a visually sensible chassis center/pivot; avoid wheel/ground pop, exaggerated translation, chassis clipping, disappearing car or steering wheel moving the entire 3D car off the collision position.
- Record actual **rendered body yaw degrees** and audio-skid state for low/moderate/hard/max scenarios. Do not mark pass only because a config max is 60°; show it happening in browser footage.
- Body animation must not affect `roadRelativeX`, longitudinal progress, off-road penalties or physics collision. Use the exact existing steering levels (N=4 detents) and rapidly responding Arcade Lateral Balance model. Do not make core steering more twitchy.
- The chase camera stays fixed in FOV and base distance regardless of speed; preserve large car, clear forward road and 1.75× sensation.

## P0-B: High-speed sweeping corners versus brake-required technical corners

The game should offer **two different rewards**, with distinct geometry and required steering demands:

**A. Full-speed sweeping bends (new/expanded)**
- At least ONE marked major **left-hand** and ONE major **right-hand** high-speed sweeper somewhere within a normal short loop (or replace current visually underwhelming stretches).
- At a displayed steady high speed near the game's existing top (about **220–234 km/h**, 1.75× world travel), a sufficiently skillful driver should be able to complete the sweeper **without releasing gas or using brake**, if the steering input matches the curve. Calibrate requiredSteer preferably in a controllable **~0.5–0.85** range at this speed. These are target intervals, tune within the existing four-notch hierarchy.
- Unlike a timid gentle bend, each should produce **substantial cumulative road-direction change**, e.g. roughly **70–110° heading change** spread over a visible longer run at high speed, without turning into an abrupt short-radius impossible hairpin. Tune length, curvature and width together while keeping player sightlines.
- Make the bend visibly dramatic in the third-person chase view: a continuous sweeping road wrapping around coast/cliff/crest is welcome, but do not require new biome or props. Visible 3D road shape must match real curve pressure and player difficulty.
- An expert maintaining high average speed feels rewarded. Human driving must not become harder only because the image was exaggerated with fake background bending.

**B. Technical/Extreme bend (preserve)**
- At original reference high speed ~230 km/h, full ±1.0 correction remains insufficient for at least one **visibly tighter** technical section; advance braking/coasting makes it controllable at a documented reduced speed.
- The required steer should be derived from real curvature using the **same shared pressure/authority mapping**, not by segment-specific invisible difficulty coefficients.
- Large-sweeper curvature can be less extreme than the technical bend, but **longer angular sweep makes the road visually spectacular**. The player must be able to distinguish the two by shape and warning cues.
- Preserve existing Low/Medium/High/S segments and existing generated-track inverse design. Author track difficulty **first**; don't just draw a huge-looking 3D curve disconnected from the model.
- Smooth all segment transitions, continuous terrain/mesh/shoulders and safe look-ahead at 1.75×, with no mountain occlusion. Keep at least a brief breathing straight.

**Calibration examples only:** A long arc with integrated heading change ~90° at radius ≈400–500 world meters may read dramatically at 1.75× after several seconds. Use the actual game unit scale and actual pressure curve to derive production values; this is *not* a mandatory geometry constant.

## P1: Create the first original open-top sports car

Only proceed when P0 handling, visuals, challenge coverage and tests actually pass. It is okay to finish v0.5 with an honest P1 defer rather than sacrificing the accepted fun.

- Replace boxy closed-roof player car with a recognizable stylish **original unbranded convertible** suitable for vivid sunlit coastal arcade style, **without copying the Ferrari Testarossa or SEGA art**.
- Requirements: genuinely roofless silhouette; visible cockpit and seats; at least **driver and passenger** recognizable in chase view; visually legible body proportions, rear bumper, wheels, lights and paint at Pixel 8 scale. No photorealism or expensive detailed modelling.
- Make the car feel designed rather than assembled from a few naked cubes: coherent body shaping, windscreen, open cabin, proper lighting, materials and the retained ~20–25% screen-width silhouette.
- Passengers are part of the spectacle and should remain recognizable during strong 50–60° visual drift without obstructing the view. Preserve original art provenance and bundled local resources. The model should be easy to replace with a better asset later.
- **BONUS ONLY:** small playful leaning or head turns driven by existing steering demand, crest or speed; optional hair/scarf flutter. Avoid complex skeletal rigs, copied character likenesses, excessive motion or drop in phone FPS.
- If roofless car quality falls below present design, correct it rather than replacing a playable car with an unfinished prototype. Maintain performance.
