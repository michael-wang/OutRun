## Soft steering

The existing Schmitt quantizer still selects neutral and ±0.25/0.50/0.75/1.00; dead zone 0.08 and hysteresis 0.025 are unchanged. The strip cursor follows raw input continuously. `src/steering.ts` adds a separate finite cubic actuator `3t²−2t³`, taking 100ms to a held target or 60ms to zero. A new target begins at the current applied value. It is a control transition, never stored physical lateral momentum.

The navigation integrator uses the exact mean actuator output over each timestep, rather than integrating only its endpoint. This makes step/release displacement stable across 30/60/120Hz. At maximum speed the touch-release tail is below 0.37m and ends after 60ms; then a straight holds its existing nonzero offset exactly. Resume clears stale actuator state. The quantized target and applied value are separately inspectable in the development facade/readout.

The hard/soft comparison uses the same current medium bend, entry speed and trusted touch schedule. The development-only hard-detent switch reproduces v0.4.2's instantaneous correction; it is not a claim that the whole v0.4.2 renderer/geometry is running. Normal production gameplay cannot enable that switch. The baseline v0.4.2 screenshots/telemetry remain preserved in their original evidence directory.

## Honest curve calibration

The pressure gain was reduced globally from **1.171823 to 0.609453**, allowing **1.923× greater physical curvature at the same reference steering demand**. There are no per-segment force multipliers. Authority remains `12 * v / vRef`; pressure is `gain * k * v²`; required steering is pressure/authority. Pace remains 1.75 and only scales longitudinal travel.

Authored demand still generates the actual mesh inversely. The signed challenge-time integral fixes a half-turn and the one global curvature-per-steer coefficient; pressure gain is derived from that coefficient and the reference speed. Low now turns right, Medium left, High/Extreme right; S remains left→right. High and Extreme are 4.5 reference seconds each, retaining 2.5s testable cores and 1s cosine ramps. Rebalancing signs and lengths makes stronger geometry close without self-crossing. Nominal course duration is 70s and length 7,826.389m; a stationary-start deterministic lap takes 74.6s. The two half-turns close without a position warp.

## Expressive body pose without path effects

`src/handling.ts` derives cinematic yaw from the shared applied input and real curve demand. Mild ≤0.25 correction has zero target yaw/skid cue; 0.5 matched correction reaches about 17.5°, High about 35°, and loaded full lock about 42°. A nonlinear smooth curve and 80ms visual easing (60ms exit recovery) avoid mechanical notch snapping. Strong steering on a straight does not produce a fake dramatic drift. The existing audio persistence/hysteresis requires sustained meaningful load; mild curves stay quiet.

Yaw, lean and skid cues do not feed s/x navigation. The collision envelope is now explicitly road-relative (2.26m wide), rather than rotating with cinematic yaw. This is necessary so large visual poses cannot activate an invisible gameplay penalty. Unit tests deliberately corrupt rendered heading while verifying identical position, speed and off-road state. The visual car can extend farther sideways during a large pose; it does not create a new collision simulation. Outer ±9.6m contact still holds until deliberate inward correction, with off-road drag and forward progress preserved.
