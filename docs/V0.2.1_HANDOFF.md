## Changes

- Gameplay buttons suppress native touch defaults, selection, drag, context menus and WebKit callouts. The prevention is scoped to controls; Settings retains ordinary selection and touch behavior. Existing independent pointer-ID handling covers release, cancel, lost capture, blur and visibility loss.
- Car centers are bounded at road-relative ±9.6m (asphalt edges ±7m). The 2.6m shoulder allowance is slightly wider than one vehicle width to accommodate a rotated car entirely outside asphalt.
- As explicitly approved during this task, outer-boundary contact persists until deliberate inward steering. Contact stops further yaw accumulation, holds lateral position and preserves forward course progress at at least 35% of current speed before the existing curvature denominator. Existing heading is never snapped or aligned. Off-road drag remains active; it settles near 31.5m/s under held gas rather than stopping the car.
- Camera follows the road heading during contact, with its existing smooth angular response, fixed FOV/distance and car-relative translation. Normal road/shoulder dynamics and camera remain unchanged before contact. Inward steering releases contact; no lane centering, bounce, reset or extra penalty was added.
- Visual testing exposed guardrail overlap. The rail now sits outside the corridor at -12.8m, the flat sea-side shoulder extends to -13m, and inland shrubs start beyond the vehicle envelope.
