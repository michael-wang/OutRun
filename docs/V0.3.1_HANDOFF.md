## Diagnosis from source and measurements

No hidden movement bottleneck was found in the tested v0.3 pipeline. At a displayed ~234 km/h the rendered car actually travels ~64.92 world units per real second. Camera translation is ~64.84 units/s, not a slower moving reference frame. The main weakness identified for this experiment was low-contrast, thin and relatively distant near-field references, rather than falsified speed or slow spline progress.

- **Scale:** 1 world unit = 1 metre; road width 14 m, original car footprint 2.26 × 4.7 m, three visual lanes ~4.67 m wide. No world/car/road rescaling was introduced.
- **Speed/caps:** `src/handling.ts:11` caps speed at 65 m/s. `src/main.ts:88` displays `round(speed * 3.6)`. Acceleration remains the v0.3 formula, with low-speed launch boost; brake subtracts 34 m/s² and off-road drag remains `10 + speed * .07`. No physics changes were made in this milestone.
- **Road travel:** `src/handling.ts:53` integrates Frenet progress `speed * cos(velocityHeading) / (1 - curvature*lateral)`, with the existing boundary projection. Turning/sliding therefore explains small differences between track progress and world-path distance. `src/track.ts:9` uses a 6,000-division arc-length lookup and `getPointAt`; the complete 4,215.27 m track is static geometry, not recycled independently of the vehicle.
- **Time:** `src/main.ts:92` integrates requestAnimationFrame elapsed time in ≤1/120 s substeps. The 0.05 s limit in the handling function is not reached by those substeps. A frame stall exceeding 0.25 s is intentionally capped; normal 30/60 FPS travel is unaffected. Background visibility pauses play. No claim of wall-time fidelity during long stalls is made.
- **Camera:** `src/main.ts:83` anchors translation directly to the car; yaw alone smooths at rate 7/s. `src/chase.ts` retains 8 m rear offset, 2.6 m height, and 76° horizontal FOV. Vertical FOV is 47.4484° at 16:9 and 38.7412° at 20:9. These values depend on aspect, never speed.

## Focused changes

`src/world.ts` changes stationary geometry, not independent scenery animation: lane dashes now repeat every 6 m instead of 12 m, are 3 m long and 0.28 m wide; alternating cream/rust edge bands change colour every 3 m. Reflectors remain 12 m apart, grow from 0.22 × 1.3 m to 0.38 × 1.6 m with dark contrast bands, and move from ±12.9 m to ±12.4 m. Edge bands are merged geometry; posts remain instances. Car, track route, steering and camera composition are retained.

Wind gain now follows the actual 65 m/s maximum rather than a 78 m/s reference and rises to 0.13 instead of ~0.069 at maximum speed. Engine pitch/throttle and slip-driven skid behavior are preserved. Audio-state tests pass; listening quality remains UNVERIFIED.

`src/speed-audit.ts` records roughly one-second windows of actual rendered world-path distance, track delta, road widths/s, camera travel, heading/position, wall and simulated time, FPS/frame intervals and interpolated stationary landmark-plane crossings. Inspect `window.__drive.audit()` in development. The ring is bounded to 120 windows and resets on pause/reset. Loop-seam marker positions are handled explicitly. This instrumentation and the comparison UI/test driver are absent from the production bundle.

Pass events are crossings of actual, regularly spaced stationary landmark planes. They are an objective near-field proxy, not a computer-vision count of every visible post.
