# v0.7A — Challenge Brick Lab

**Milestone status:** USER AUTHORIZED for implementation.  
**Base:** The EXISTING PLAYABLE LOCAL v0.6 code (`6401a03` implementation, `cf83901` handoff reported; inspect actual HEAD).  
**Single product question:** Can eight *meaningfully different* challenge concepts be experienced, evaluated, replayed and curated as building blocks for later journey generation?

## 1. Outcome the owner can actually play

At `http://<PC-LAN-IP>:5173/?lab=bricks` on Pixel 8 (and localhost on PC), expose a simple, legible **Brick Lab** rather than the old v0.6 free drive. It needs:
- Eight distinct candidate challenge cards with BRK-01..BRK-08, a short player-facing title and ONE SENTENCE about what to attempt; no lengthy design jargon.
- **Play / Replay Same / Next** (and ideally Previous), simple candidate selector and visible candidate ID+revision. A candidate should be repeatable in ~15–40 seconds *including* an approach, main moment and pleasant exit; choose actual lengths by reference speed and fairness.
- **KEEP / REWORK / REJECT**, plus an optional one-line note. Buttons don't interrupt an active drive accidentally; show review controls before/after a drive or in a small pause/review view. Review should work by touch/keyboard; don't obscure steering/throttle/brake.
- A compact review progress indicator showing **unreviewed, kept, rework, reject** counts, separate from automated QA results.
- **Persistent local reviews** (`localStorage` or similarly trivial client storage), versioned by candidate ID+revision. A revision change does NOT inherit old approval silently. Provide an **Export Reviews** JSON download/copy; never require an account, backend or paid provider.
- A clear way to return to ordinary v0.6 play. A lab-mode URL must never force normal players into developer review UI.
- If Codex can't expose a live server for phone access, provide an exact `npm run dev -- --host 0.0.0.0` command and real reachable URL as observed, not a guessed LAN IP.

**Important:** A test environment must NEVER auto-click KEEP on the owner's actual persisted review storage. Initial shipped state is UNREVIEWED. The user's own KEEP vote is the explicit human acceptance signal; until then a brick remains a candidate even if tests say PASS.

## 2. Eight concrete candidates: all must be REAL driveable scenes

Implement the eight designs in `docs/CHALLENGE_BRICK_CANDIDATES.md`, using the EXISTING road and scenic capabilities only:

| ID | Candidate | Unique play feel / actual test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BRK-01 | Coastal Panorama Sweeper | Hold near-full throttle through a visually huge but actually controllable left/right sweeper; excitement from uninterrupted pace and ocean vista. |
| BRK-02 | Double-S Rhythm | Move steering across zero through connected left-right changes; reward timing and a clean neutral exit. |
| BRK-03 | Crest & Reveal | Climb/crest then readable curving descent; preserve minimum sightline/warning, reward a landscape reveal. |
| BRK-04 | Canyon Brake Test | Visibly tight, full-steering insufficient near 230 km/h; player can anticipate and BRAKE to pass the SAME corner. |
| BRK-05 | Downhill Acceleration Reward | Strong descending road, near-road scenery flow and forgiving sweeper; reward speed after a climb/technical beat. |
| BRK-06 | Edge Discipline | Moderate curvature, narrower visible asphalt and close canyon walls/guardrail visuals; recoverable offroad; decor has NO unimplemented collision. |
| BRK-07 | City Boulevard Slalom | Distinctly longer spaced boulevard bends with original city foreground, different tempo from tight Double-S. |
| BRK-08 | Harbor Hairpin & Relief | Readable short brake-required turn followed by *obvious* wide high-speed payoff and scenic vista; different emotion from BRK-04. |

The eight must have separate, recognizable visual and steering/speed/grade profiles, not eight labels on identical road parameters. They can reuse shared primitives and art; do not build collision cars, cones or roadblock hazards in this milestone.

For each candidate create an **authored gameplay data definition**, with at minimum:
`id`, `revision`, `name`, `intent`, `emotionalRole`, `sceneryAct`, `seed`, `referenceSpeed`, `requiredSteerProfile` (signed/smooth), `length/nominalDuration`, `roadWidth`, `grade/elevation`, `entry/exit` constraints, `warning/lookahead`, `tags` and `antiAdjacency` where meaningful, plus `autoValidation` status tracked separately from `humanReview`.

Derived curvature/geometry **must flow from the shared Required Steering→curve pressure inverse calibration**, not have an arbitrary label stuck on pre-made geometry. Use existing procedural 3D track/scenery and lifecycle, not a new renderer or physics library.

## 3. Isolated challenge approach and handling

Every candidate should include **brief approach → readable challenge → exit/relief**. Build/recycle enough 3D roadway ahead/behind to avoid gaps or camera/terrain clipping when starting from a candidate. If the ordinary game loops, lab challenges should have a clear `Completed`/`Replay` point and may offer Next; don't require laps/leaderboards. A candidate can be failed by going off asphalt but the game should allow recovery or quick replay without long off-road detours.

Use stable per-candidate `seed` and generator/version ID. Identical candidate + revision + seed + vehicle/config must produce identical track segment profiles and scenery placements, including on reload. Do not call unseeded `Math.random` in generated content.

## 4. Mechanical fairness and context

- **BRK-01** at nominal high pace has a full-gas, steer-correct success path.
- **BRK-02** and **BRK-07** have distinctly different curvature cadence/segment spacing, and an actual steer→reverse→settle sequence can be driven.
- **BRK-03** gives sufficient preview around crest; never hide a mandatory brake apex without warning.
- **BRK-04** and **BRK-08** provide brake-warning distance/visible curvature appropriate for their nominal speed; high-speed full-lock can fail on intended brake-required segments but early braking allows a real success.
- **BRK-05** feels like acceleration on a strong downhill, not a flat renamed straight.
- **BRK-06** is only a narrow-road *perceptual* challenge using existing off-road slowdown, not fake collision with props.
- Camera look-ahead, road width, shoulder limits, scenery overlap and curvature joins remain sound. No sudden impossible kinks, disappearing road, road-edge center snap or physical yaw inertia.
- Keep support for opposite left/right mirrored variants where appropriate, but **one canonical replayable instance per ID** is enough for v0.7A.

## 5. What deliberately stays OUT

- No five-minute Pacific Road Trip **yet**. The v0.7B composition of a full journey is gated by the owner's brick reviews. No mystery seed journey, route forks, RPG money/upgrades, garage, selectable cars, adaptive difficulty, physical hazards, opponents or large Stage Composer.
- No new snow/volcano art; only the current Azure Coast, Golden Canyon, Sunlit Coastal City resources.
- No online accounts or storage, telemetry upload, public deployment, new game engine or giant abstract authoring framework.
- Do not alter accepted 1.75× pace, Arcade Lateral Balance, steering levels/hand controls, car animation, audio and camera physics to make a candidate pass. Revise **the candidate** instead.
