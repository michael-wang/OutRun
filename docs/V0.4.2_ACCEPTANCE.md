### Required recorded response sequences

In same track section at same initial speed and pose, record **steeringTarget, commandYaw, yawRate, carHeading, velocityHeading/slipAngle, trackTangent, roadLateralOffset, offRoad, speed**.

- Short 150ms left → 400ms neutral and mirrored right. Check onset and yaw/slip decay, separate vehicle heading from road center heading.
- Full input ~800ms into actual bend → reduce/countersteer 150–300ms → 500ms neutral, mirror directions. Tune to realistic player action; do not assert every arbitrary input will stay on-road.
- Phone finger slides 20%→50%→80% on the same touch and releases while accelerator stays held independently.
- Browser recordings MUST demonstrate the curve entry, exit, rebound/overshoot potential and corrective action at **1.75×**. A state test without visible motion is insufficient.

## Visual/mobile/hardware regression

- Analog needle+number remains physically **left of car**, doesn't obstruct phone steering strip or swapped pedal group; desktop 1280×720 & Pixel-like 960×432, 844×390.
- Car screen width ~20–25% in landscape, fixed camera FOV/distance independent of speed; speed remains exciting on phone and PC.
- Mobile fullscreen/PWA/orientation/cutouts, pointer capture/lost/cancel and 15s held gas+steering unchanged.
- Build, unit, Playwright pass; no new uncaught console errors; only report actual Pixel FPS and audio review if device/listening was used.

## Failure modes that are NOT acceptable

- "Exit stability" achieved by snapping car heading to spline or magnetically returning it to lane center.
- "Harder corners" achieved by more twitchy input, constant near-90° road kinks or impossible blind crest turns.
- "More hills" implemented only as distant hill models without actual road elevation.
- Long drive test passes numerical progress but road visually ends/gaps, or renders beneath mountain.
- All tests pass but real video still shows the opposite-shoulder lurch.
