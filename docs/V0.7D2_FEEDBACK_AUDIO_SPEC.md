## P0-A — genuine high-load skid audio, not every lateral movement

### Diagnose first

Read real v0.7D.1 skid trigger. It reportedly added "actual lateral movement as an additional symmetric squeal trigger". In Arcade Lateral Balance, `dx/dt` can be nonzero simply because the player steers across road; **ordinary lateral correction ≠ tire grip failure**. Investigate whether this causes low-speed squeals, and inspect speed thresholds, signed left/right mapping, corner load, animation cues, pause/replay and envelope resets before editing.

### Desired audible behavior

- Gentle low-speed bends and lane adjustments should be QUIET even with lateral road-relative displacement. At roughly **60–100 km/h**, most ordinary corrections never squeal. Severe deliberate high-load situations at somewhat lower speed MAY squeal if clear; do not hard-code a giant absolute ban without testing. Tune as a continuous combination of meaningful curve load, actual mismatch/slip, speed and sustained effort, with modest hysteresis; a rising floor/gain envelope with speed is fine. The accepted car steering and grip mathematics should NOT be rewritten to fix audio.
- At same physical/signed turn intensity, left/right drift produces comparable audible effects. No intermittent missing left squeal, but also no blanket sound on both sides. Tire-skid SFX should correspond to visibly dramatic corners, not all movement/quantizer changes.
- Make generated skid sound clearly LESS shrill: reduce excessive upper band/whistle or narrow resonant spikes, reduce oscillator/filter Q and/or employ gentler bandlimited noise and low/mid texture where appropriate; tune based on ACTUAL current Web Audio graph, do not blindly pick EQ values before inspection.
- **Squeal must not dominate the engine.** During simultaneous engine+skid, target a conservative skid bus (initial guide ~6–9 dB lower short-term RMS than engine in comparable full-throttle corners, then human listen/tune; RMS alone cannot guarantee perceptual balance). Apply separate gain ceiling, smooth attack/release and avoid clipping or harsh onset. Preserve the OWNER-PASSED warm RPM/gear engine timbre unchanged.
- Actual browser audio before/after capture and multiple low/medium/high stress left/right turns; report measured skid/engine audio mix levels, peak/no clipping, and subjective real speaker/headphone listening as UNVERIFIED unless personally done. Keep mute/user-gesture unlock and sound on/off.

## P0-D — RETROSPECTIVE event anchor (must fix timing!)

**A quick positive reaction usually refers to a turn/hill/reveal already passed**. As soon as L1/R1 or G/I is pressed, capture reaction time AND select an actual RECORDED recent driving snapshot, **not a fabricated retroactive value**.

- Keep a small in-memory circular buffer of **last ~8–10 seconds of SIMULATED DRIVING time** at modest sampling resolution (e.g. ~10Hz). Snapshots: game/route/Stage/L2 ID+revision+seed, `s`/world distance, elapsed simulated seconds, world phase, speed, steer, x, grade, width, active primitive/profile IDs, meaningful skid/curve load if available. Clip to route transition; handle pauses/restarts and L2 completion safely. No continuous audio/video recording and no network upload.
- On feedback invocation, default the marker to an **actual earlier snapshot around ~2–3 seconds before keypress**. Show label such as `📍 2 秒前（s=...）`, not current position. If route just started, clamp to earliest available genuine sample and disclose offset.
- Allow player to adjust chosen snapshot among recent options **Now / -2s / -4s / -6s / -8s** (or nearest actual samples). Keyboard `[`/`]` or simple on-screen arrows; optional gamepad **Square/Triangle** move anchor older/newer WHILE modal is open, only if they do not collide with issue shortcut; map consciously with visible hints. D-pad remains reserved for four praise tags. If only one option exists, don't claim selectable history.
- Default should reference the immediately prior meaningful drive, not necessarily the just-finished centerline position. At L2/Stage completion retain last buffer briefly so `超難! 但是過了超爽!` can attach to the passed challenging bend, not only the finish line. Past snapshot includes its OWN world and L1 IDs even if player crossed biome boundary.
- When Saved, export both `reactionAt{time,s}` and `eventAt{time,s}` (chosen genuine historical sample), `offsetSeconds`, tag/note, controller/keyboard origin and route/geometry fingerprint. Maintain old schema readers and historical exported user files; use separate schema version or optional new fields, do not mutate past review JSON.
- Preserve ability to flag a problem immediately and leave a short issue note. If app is paused during feedback, the small history buffer must not slide away or keep driving.
- Only persist deliberate user events, not entire 10s buffer or raw player audio/video. localStorage and JSON Export Feedback only, no login/server/telemetry. Multiple praises at same location remain allowed and distinguishable.
