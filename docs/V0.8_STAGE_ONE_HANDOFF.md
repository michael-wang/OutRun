# v0.8 — Stage 01: Pacific Getaway handoff

Branch `codex/v0.8-stage-one`, based on the playable local `71476fb`. Implementation and evidence commit: `f808ccd`. The stage is an alpha for human playtesting; no claim of player approval is implied.

## Start locally

```powershell
cd C:\astra-dev\OutRun
npm ci
npm run dev -- --host 0.0.0.0
```

PC: <http://localhost:5173/?stage=1>. Pixel on the same Wi-Fi: <http://192.168.1.105:5173/?stage=1>. The LAN address was reached from the host; a physical phone connection was not tested and the address can change.

The stage starts from rest. Enter a numeric seed or the complete Stage Code, use Play, Replay Same, or New Route. P starts/resumes, R replays, N chooses a new route. During driving, G opens praise and I opens an issue. With a supported standard PS4 mapping, L1 or R1 opens praise; D-pad selects, Cross saves and resumes, Circle cancels, Triangle changes historical time and Square immediately flags an issue for later editing in the review panel. The displayed prompts follow the last meaningful input. Feedback stays local and exports the stage code, actual earlier event and reaction snapshots, world, beat and profiles.

## Generation and evidence

Stage codes have the form `S01-v0.8-r1-<base36 seed>-ALB1`. The generator builds eight distance-localized C/E/W beats, with a city-to-coast-to-canyon arc, seeded turns and magnitudes, real lane-width and height changes, and seeded reveal locations. Elevation beats finish before biome boundaries; the canyon is more likely to receive a dramatic amplitude, but city/coast exceptions remain possible. A deterministic clearance check rejects candidates whose distant road sections would overlap the broad terrain corridor. The finite stage's road and terrain ribbons use independently culled sections. Existing pilots, physics, powertrain and audio were not changed.

The five-seed timeline and sampled geometry are in `docs/evidence/v0.8/five-seed-timelines.json`; the 100-seed measured amplitude distribution is in `biome-distribution.json`. Dramatic beats: city 19/245, coast 64/307, canyon 115/248. In addition to the structural checks, browser rays sampled the five routes at 250 m intervals for road surface and overhead scenery/terrain. `dramatic-coast.png` and `dramatic-canyon.png` show seed 3's actual exceptional coastal and canyon profiles. `touch-844.png`, `touch-960.png`, `stage-menu.png` and `gamepad-drive.png` show representative layouts.

`seed-1-full-drive.webm`, `seed-2-full-drive.webm`, and `seed-3-full-drive.webm` are full real-time browser recordings from standing start to finish. They use a test-only scripted driver through the actual game's control interface, not a human player or teleporting between views. Playwright's video capture is silent; it is not evidence of a human audio evaluation. Their traces and charts are alongside them as `seed-*-drive.json` and `seed-*-chart.svg`. Simulated times were 128.90, 129.29 and 129.83 seconds, respectively, with no browser errors. Each averaged about 60 rendered frames/s on this desktop during capture; the scripted driver stayed on the road and its steering/braking traces differ across seeds. Physical phone performance remains unverified.

## Tests and limitations

The production build passes with Vite's bundle-size advisory. `npm run test` passes 83 tests in 27 files. `$env:OUTRUN_BROWSER='msedge'; npm run test:e2e -- --workers=2` passes 100 tests in 3.7 minutes; two subsequently added browser tests for rendered replay determinism and the valley view pass separately. Logs are `unit-results.txt`, `e2e-results.txt` and `extra-results.txt` in the evidence directory. The browser test also traverses the stage twice and checks that renderer geometry and texture counts stabilize after initial visibility uploads. Browser controller tests use a mocked standard gamepad and touch tests use trusted simulated touches. Physical PS4 and Pixel 8 input, sustained phone performance, audio, and whether the routes are fun remain UNVERIFIED pending the owner's playtest. No traffic, additional stages, online service or automatic progression is included.
