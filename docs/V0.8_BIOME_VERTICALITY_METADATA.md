# v0.8 — Biome-aware verticality metadata for Stage One

**Product decision 28 October 2026:** The owner prefers the MOST DRAMATIC road elevation in **Grand Canyon / mountains**, whereas city and seaside driving typically feel more natural with gentle or rolling hills. **This is a SOFT GENERATION PREFERENCE, NOT A BAN.** Approved player feedback also marks a real steep coast counter-bend and coastal rolling S-turn as thrilling, so it would be wrong to prohibit those combinations outright.

## Correct ownership

L1 remains one of C curvature / E elevation / W width primitive profile families. L2 is a short driving experience made of locally placed overlapping L1 events. **L3 biome/world metadata** owns environment identity and the preferences affecting which L1 elevation *parameters/instances* are selected. The Stage generator consults L3 metadata when it samples an L2's elevation and location; Terrain and Scenery then fit actual road geometry and biome.

**Current supported worlds:** city, coast, canyon. Mountain/alpine remains future, not a playable v0.8 art pack. Do not create made-up snow assets.

## First lightweight data-driven preference

A small typed object in content/config code is enough; no generic rule engine. Initial **illustrative RELATIVE weights** by visual verticality class:

| L3 | gentle | rolling | dramatic | Typical visual logic |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| city | 5 | 4 | 1 | Broad urban boulevard, elevated ramps/viaducts can justify occasional big elevation. |
| coast | 3 | 5 | 2 | Gentle shore, broad waterfront hills; cliff coast can have spectacular exceptional descent. |
| canyon | 1 | 4 | 5 | Big canyon ridge climb, valley floor, long steep descents, dramatic crest/reveal are common. |
| mountain (future) | 1 | 3 | 6 | Prepared only, not used until an alpine environment actually exists. |

**These are starting weights, not mandatory tuned constants.** Codex should derive/adjust simple tiers using the game's existing world-height, distance/grade, camera perception and user-approved BRK/L2 pilot examples. Do not classify an entire E03 shape "dramatic" just from its ID: one small crest and one giant crest share a shape family; consider its actual altitude delta, grade profile and visible duration. Preserve user-accepted 300kmh driving physics, never invent a per-biome fake handling/grip bonus/penalty.

### Minimal implementation sketch (conceptual)

`type Verticality = 'gentle' | 'rolling' | 'dramatic';

type WorldMeta = {
  id: 'city' | 'coast' | 'canyon';
  verticalityWeights: Record<Verticality, number>;
  terrainPalette: string;      // EXISTING scenery/terrain pack
  surface: 'paved';            // current physics
};`

Procedural stage:
1. Establish coherent stage thematic world sequence/phase first.
2. Sample **verticality** per L2 beat/section according to that phase's WorldMeta weights using the stage seed (not `Math.random`).
3. Pick/parameterize an Elevation primitive compatible with that desired tier and local road segment length. Curvature & Width remain independently designed but compose with that E profile; preserve meaningful multi-event L2 placement schedule.
4. Derive road real 3D geometry. Terrain/scenery align with actual geometry, grade and visible corridor. Grand canyon gets terrain cross sections with cliff/ridge silhouette; coastal exceptional elevation needs plausible headland/cliff/bridge with continuous sea level, city dramatic elevation needs coherent ramp/viaduct terrain/buildings. Do not simply recolor the same huge floating slope.
5. Keep transition grades, height and centerline continuous when a city→coast→canyon scene phase changes; NO sudden elevation "jump" merely because L3 switched. Local grade profiles must remain smooth.
6. Seed code + generator version reproduces same biome/elevation association; changing purely cosmetic scenery randomization must not change curvature/grade.

## Lightweight validation

- For a small **batch of reproducible sample seeds** (e.g. 20–30 fast deterministic generation calls), the distribution of **dramatic sections** should be **relatively higher in canyon than city**, generally also than coast. Do not require EVERY canyon section to be a cliff nor EVERY coast route to be flat.
- At least one **non-dramatic canyon** and one **dramatic coast or city exception** remains technically constructible with supported real terrain; demonstrate at least one exception in deterministic generation tests without forcing it into every human stage.
- Verify actual grade/elevation distribution and BIOME metadata source, not just labels. Visual-check road/terrain continuity at a dramatic canyon crest and any exceptional coastal grade in rendered browser.
- The owner is the final judge of natural/scenic fit and fun. Existing individually-KEEP pilot/BRK routes are preserved *unchanged*; use new Stage seed/version for any reweighted generated road.

### Out of scope

No natural-language AI biome planner, no 3D editor, complicated optimize-every-seed feasibility solver, climate/snow/gravel physics, or real mountains pack. This is just a content metadata bias that can be tuned after first Stage One human seed ratings.
