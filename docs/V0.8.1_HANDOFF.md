## Powertrain evidence

The model now uses gear ratios tied to the RPM/speed calibration, an RPM-dependent engine torque band, a low-gear traction cap, quadratic aerodynamic resistance and the existing integrated grade, braking and off-road forces. The engine audio graph and handling model remain unchanged. The 0.18-second torque interruption and the road-speed response are integrated as forces; no fixed speed is subtracted on a shift.

Flat 60 fps actual simulation, incremental seconds per 100 km/h:

| Version | 0–100 | 100–200 | 200–300 | 0–300 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| v0.8 | 4.53 | 6.30 | 10.80 | 21.63 |
| v0.8.1 | 3.37 | 5.92 | 13.53 | 22.82 |

Mean acceleration by gears 1–6, excluding shifts and the speed cap, changed from approximately 7.00/6.22/5.25/4.29/3.29/2.25 to 9.19/9.11/6.46/4.32/2.91/1.70 m/s². In the new flat 60 fps trace, each shift reaches zero wheel traction and negative net acceleration; the fourth and fifth shifts have measured speed drops of approximately 0.13 and 0.31 km/h from their pre-shift samples. The full 30/60 fps, uphill and coast traces are `powertrain-before.json` and `powertrain-after.json`; the real rendered browser traces are `browser-powertrain-before.json` and `browser-powertrain-after.json`. `acceleration-before-after.svg`, `five-shifts.svg` and `powertrain-summary.json` provide charts and shift-by-shift measurements. Earlier code used near-equal gear multipliers rather than physical gear ratios, so no retroactive baseline ratio is invented.

## Stage composition and width

Each new L2 core has two successive local curvature events and overlapping elevation, with four-lane defaults and selected real width transitions. L3 owns explicitly annotated launch, scenic, braking, optional short connector and finish buffers. Curvature, elevation and widths remain distance-based and reproducible. At 5-unit sampling intervals, stable lanes and tapers are measured separately; three-lane geometry is deferred rather than describing a taper as a stable three-lane road.

Window coverage is not a claim that every instant inside a window has nonzero steering demand. `stage-metrics.json` records the actual core, buffer and placement timelines, resolved widths and gaps for all five seeds.

The three `seed-*-full-drive.webm` files show complete real-time browser runs from rest to finish, with actual stage code, clock and speed. ## Validation and remaining human gate

The production build passes with Vite's bundle-size advisory. `npm run test` passes 86 tests in 30 files; `$env:OUTRUN_BROWSER='msedge'; npm run test:e2e -- --workers=2` passes all 104 browser tests in 3.5 minutes. Logs are `unit-results.txt`, `e2e-results.txt` and `focused-results.txt`. The full suite covers 30/60 fps motion, five force-based shifts, grade, sound-state regression, controller feedback, phone touch layouts, five-seed rendered road probes, real width/paint/off-road checks and unchanged historical review stores. The existing uphill test reaches 300 km/h at approximately 29.3 seconds; its former 28-second bound was updated to reflect the requested weaker high-gear acceleration, while flat 0–300 remains within the target.

Browser gamepad tests use a mocked standard controller and phone tests use simulated trusted touch; physical PS4, Pixel 8, actual speaker/headphone audio and subjective driving feel remain UNVERIFIED. No Stage 02, new biome, traffic, editor, deployment or online service is included.
