## Changes and measured handling

Default world pace remains 1.75; displayed speed remains the existing arcade km/h gauge. The camera retains its fixed FOV and dolly geometry. No steering or lane-centering assistance was added.

Digital steering builds authority with a smooth 0.85-second hold ramp. Reversal resets that ramp; release relaxes it. A full hold retains strong cornering authority (maximum base yaw rate 0.65 rad/s). The ramp, rather than a global speed reduction, removes the abrupt tap response.

Straight, indicated 234 km/h, 1.75 pace, fixed 120 Hz integration:

| Hold | v0.3.2 yaw / lateral | v0.4 yaw / lateral |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 180 ms (22 ticks) | 8.46° / 0.386 m | 1.16° / 0.064 m |
| 700 ms | 31.97° / 9.6 m (boundary) | 17.18° / 5.408 m |
| 1 second | 31.97° / 9.6 m (boundary) | 25.81° / 9.6 m (boundary) |

Both directions are symmetric. Sustained steering still intentionally leaves a straight road; it is not lane keeping. `tests/fixtures/v032-handling.ts` preserves the prior integrator for a reproducible comparison. Raw results: `evidence/v0.4/steering-before-after.json`.

Drift uses actual body/velocity separation and reduced grip only after strong sustained steering (>0.85 smoothed authority, >35 m/s). Release/countersteer restores grip. Squeal starts after >100 ms of meaningful slip (roughly 19° at speed) and uses a separate quiet threshold/persistence to avoid chatter. The final real-time recording reached **29.64°** and a skid gain of 0.203. It touched the shoulder, then recovered under deliberate corrective inputs: the last off-road sample was at 3.40s, and the final lateral offset was 0.085m at full speed. No audio listening or physical-device claim is made.

Speed is now a high-contrast upper-right instrument: 58px desktop / 50px phone digits, safe-area margins, compact Menu at upper left. The original vehicle and scenery assets are retained.

## Road rhythm

Distance-authored continuous curvature lobes replace the previous arbitrary spline spacing. A 3,130m rhythm repeats around a 6,260m closed coast route, with an S transition, uphill/downhill variation and the existing scenery. Elevation spans 8–96m; grades are deliberately gentler (~4.4%) for readable high-speed sightlines. Distance is horizontal track progress; the small grade adds a small difference to 3D travel distance.

| Beat | Distance | Nominal at 113.75m/s | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 0–740m | 6.51s | Open acceleration |
| B | 740–1195m | 4.00s | Sustained bend |
| C | 1195–1935m | 6.51s | Breather |
| D | 1935–2390m | 4.00s | Left/right S |
| E | 2390–2675m | 2.51s | Short danger bend |
| F | 2675–3130m | 4.00s | Recovery |

These durations are design references at full pace, not timers. Braking extends actual corner time. The deterministic full-cycle comparison completed in 35.8s with zero off-road samples for the planned digital driver, versus 143.1s and 1,355 off-road samples for gas-only/no-steering. The external test driver has been retuned for progressive steering and previews geometry for braking; it is not loaded in normal gameplay. See `evidence/v0.4/rhythm-unit.json`.

The **actual browser recording** first crossed 3,130m at **35.65s**, with no off-road samples or squeal. Recorded A–F times were approximately **6.50 / 5.00 / 6.55 / 7.35 / 5.90 / 4.30s**. This conservative driver brakes to ~23m/s in D and ~24m/s in E; those real durations are intentionally reported separately from the nominal design map. Human feedback is needed on whether the slower danger sequence is enjoyable. The recording continued to 3,238m/36.60s. See `evidence/v0.4/capture-summary.json` and `rhythm.webm`.
