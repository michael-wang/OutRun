# v0.7A.1 — Verticality & Variable Width

**Base:** Playable LOCAL v0.7A at `b6ee7ea` implementation + `e6e80f0` handoff (verify local HEAD).  
**Owner review:** Source: `docs/reviews/V0.7A_R1_HUMAN_REVIEWS.json`, exported after playing all eight r1 candidates. Six KEEP (01,02,04,06,07,08), two REWORK (03,05). Both REWORK notes: **"More radical height delta"**. Global feedback: **almost no road-width variation; show meaningful 2-lane through 8-lane roads**.

**Product decision:** Refine these eight established bricks, **not** invent eight more. Add two missing dimensions to the existing Gameplay-First authored road generator. NO five-minute assembled route in this milestone.

## 1. Design intent — increase VISUAL and gameplay diversity

Two independently controlled profiles per brick:
- **Altitude profile** `height(s)` and slope `dh/ds`. The road actually climbs/drops in Three.js world space; camera, car, roadside geometry, terrain and visibility react coherently. Drive dynamics/curvature pressure remain the human-approved model unless a documented vertical-coordinate bug needs correction.
- **Drivable-width profile** `halfRoadWidth(s)`, authored from **total laneCount** with stable lane-width constant and shoulders, then smoothly tapered between sections. The mesh, shoulder thresholds, lane markings and roadside placement all use the SAME cross-section/profile at track distance `s`. Avoid one fixed global road width hidden under painted fake lanes.

These are meaningful road/experience constraints, not new car physics, hazards or gameplay currencies. Preserve challenge-first required-steering inverse curvature design.

## 2. P0 — bolder altitude / verticality

### Required changes

- **BRK-03 Crest & Reveal** should contain a clearly perceptible substantial climb, a real dramatic skyline/roadway crest, and a longer visible valley/descending reveal. It should feel like **cresting a mountain ridge**, not a slight sidewalk bump. Make the rise, peak and fall observable from the locked follow camera, in normal high-speed travel; don't fake it by zooming FOV, raising the car, or just changing mountain meshes.
- **BRK-05 Downhill Acceleration Reward** should contain a **sustained, markedly steep downhill** with strong near-road optical flow, a visual descent into a different landscape level, and a satisfying open road exit. Strong descent, not one tiny dip hidden under the scenery.
- Add at least one secondary visually obvious elevation rhythm elsewhere in the existing eight if the user-approved gameplay tolerates it (e.g. mild coastal climb or city downhill), but **keep BRK-04 braking readability, BRK-02 Double-S timing and BRK-01 flat-out sweeper fun intact**.

### Initial tuning / real engine scale

The user specifically asked for **more radical** height differences. Do not satisfy this by changing a scalar from 1 to 1.1.

At actual game-world scale, experiment with:
- BRK-03 peak-to-valley vertical magnitude on the order of **~80–160 world units** (adapt to the real app's coordinate scale), with visually strong grades and smooth approaches;
- BRK-05 meaningful net fall on the order of **~100–200 world units**, potentially with local slopes around **8–15%** on the steep sections;
- keep slopes, duration and camera lookahead balanced: numbers are STARTING EXPERIMENTS, not mandates if they cause the car to clip, sky/terrain to become implausible, road to disappear or performance to collapse.

**Visual proof outranks numeric targets.** Record old-vs-new real chase-view clips, a road height-vs-distance trace, total rise/drop, max grade, minimum forward visible roadway distance and actual screenshots from before crest, at crest, and on steep descent. Real terrain/road edges/guard rails and nearby props must follow height; no floating bridge or mountains obscuring asphalt.

### Safety & fairness

- At ~230 km/h indicated and accepted 1.75× world pace, **BRK-03 cannot conceal an unavoidable brake-required bend immediately after a blind crest**. A dramatic reveal is fine; unfair instant steering/obstacle trap is not.
- Maintain continuous elevation and slope at joins/loop boundaries to avoid hard Z/floor jumps or camera whiplash. Prevent negative gaps, underside road viewing, prop-ground clipping and car becoming airborne accidentally (there is no requested jumping mechanic).
- Be careful when very steep pitch influences steering/camera orientation; preserve player controls and road-relative coordinate stability, no gravity-based speed changes unless approved.
- Do not use camera-only trickery to hide insufficient actual elevation. The camera should show the grade convincingly while preserving **fixed speed-independent** FOV/dolly and readable asphalt ahead.

## 3. P0 — full 2-to-8 lane road-width variation

### Semantics and geometry

Use **TOTAL visible drivable lanes** (both directions combined), first pass `laneCount ∈ {2, 4, 6, 8}`. This is sufficient meaningful coverage of the owner's 2-to-8 request; extra odd-lane layouts may be added later if they offer a distinct playable experience.

- Real width: derive `roadHalfWidth(s)` from `laneCount` × `calibratedLaneWidth` plus separately defined paved edge/shoulder as appropriate. Calibrate lane width from existing car-to-road visual scale — don't assume arbitrary Three.js world units equal real meters without checking.
- Geometric road vertex pairs, markings, asphalt width, collision/off-road test boundaries, guardrail/curb/rocks/near props and drivable recovery corridor all follow the SAME distance-based width. No illusions where 8 visible lanes still collide at the old 4-lane shoulder.
- Visually legible lane lines: two-lane rural roadway has simple center/dashed edges; 4/6/8 have appropriate multi-lane dashed lines and center division painted/visual only (no unimplemented lethal invisible median). Clear readable lane count on Pixel 8 without excessive graphics density.
- **Tapered width changes** over safe readable distance, not instantly snapped at a segment border. Initial focus at least one `2↔4` and one `4↔6↔8` transition within the test catalog, with advance visual warning before narrowing. If steering/speed cannot leave a shrinking outer lane in time, extend the taper rather than auto-centering the car or secretly widening collision.
- Width only changes *allowable lateral margin* and scenery/composition, **NOT** the curve's physics pressure, the car's steering authority, invisible traction or automatic lane-centering.
- Distinguish lanes from shoulders and road edges, keep usable paved width valid; prevent under-road painted lines and disappearing railings. At joins, lane-line transition should merge rather than teleport; use natural lane merge arrows or clear line taper if simple.
- Road-width transitions must work on road with elevation as well, not only flat land. Coordinate sign and camera collision remain coherent at high rendered body yaw.

### First authored width assignments (can adjust for better art/fairness)

| Brick | Road width target | Signature effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BRK-01 Coastal Panorama | **4→6 lanes** | Fast open coastal highway widens into panoramic cruising road; keep full-gas sweeper possible. |
| BRK-02 Double-S Rhythm | **4 lanes** | Retain known-fun precise rhythm; can remain unchanged and keep r1 only if geometry/handling truly unchanged. |
| BRK-03 Crest & Reveal | **2→4 lanes** | Narrow mountain approach broadens on/after safe crest, then valley opens. |
| BRK-04 Canyon Brake Test | **2 lanes** | Canyon technical corner visually tight; **early brake warning** remains fair. |
| BRK-05 Downhill Acceleration | **4→6 lanes** | Expansive downhill road, widening supports speed-reward spectacle. |
| BRK-06 Edge Discipline | **4→2→4 lanes** | Narrow mountain-road control with real width compression and a recoverable shoulder. |
| BRK-07 City Boulevard Slalom | **6→8→6 lanes** | Impressively wide urban boulevard, with readable lane markings and longer spaced slalom. |
| BRK-08 Harbor Hairpin & Relief | **4→2→4 lanes** | Clear signposted narrowing well BEFORE the brake corner and widening on the relief run; no instant unfair bottleneck. |

**Actual lane count and actual mesh** are acceptance requirements. One unchanged 4-lane BRK-02 is acceptable as an intentional contrast; don't mutate already-good bricks without a gameplay reason. Default free-drive can retain old width if needed; Brick Lab must demonstrate true differences.

## 4. Preserve actual first-round approvals and revision meaning

**The attached R1 feedback is a valuable human signal**, NOT permission to declare any revised geometry already KEEP.

- Archive original R1 export unmodified in `docs/reviews/V0.7A_R1_HUMAN_REVIEWS.json` and retain original candidate IDs, seeds, notes, auto QA and votes. These include 6 KEEP and 2 REWORK, not universal approval.
- For each candidate where pitch/height/width/curvature/difficulty/visibility/entry speed materially changes, create `revision=2` (or an explicit content fingerprint version) and initialize ONLY the **new revision's humanVote = UNREVIEWED**. Preserve R1 reviews in localStorage and in the archived JSON.
- A candidate with no meaningful changes (e.g. BRK-02) can retain its original r1 approval and source revision if its actual generated road fingerprint and gameplay truly match. Don't auto-upgrade merely because data catalogs are reserialized.
- Show the new revision visibly in Brick Lab; optionally show historical R1 vote as “Prior revision: KEEP / REWORK,” **not** as current approval. Don't auto-import/overwrite the user's localStorage with archived file or synthetic ratings. Allow new separate R2 export with ID/revision/seed/engine version.
- Do not mark an R2 as HUMAN_APPROVED solely because its R1 received KEEP. Auto QA is also never a substitute for owner KEEP.
- Work initially on BOTH r1 comparisons and r2 in developer testing; hand off to the user only the relevant changed revisions for review, with a quick way to replay any of the eight.

## 5. Constraints and anti-goals

Preserve from the v0.7A playable LOCAL game:
- Pixel 8 landscape/fullscreen/PWA/two-thumb/hand swap/long press; PC A/D + Space gas + Enter brake; car-left needle gauge; pause/audio.
- Body yaw/character hair/wheel animation and existing 3 scenic themes; accepted 1.75× pace, 4 soft steering detents, direct Arcade Lateral Balance, brake-required vs full-speed corners, bounded off-road penalty/recovery.
- Existing `?lab=bricks` menu and normal free-drive; review persistence and JSON export; no auto human reviews.
- Current inverse Required Steering→3D curvature: turn directions/steering calibration should not be altered to make road width/grades "work." Use track/scenery/width authoring data, not fake camera speed or hidden physics.

Do NOT develop the 5-minute Pacific Road Trip, Mystery Drive, Stage Composer/editor, economy, coins, purchases, garages, extra car art, traffic, rivals, actual collision roadblocks, snow, volcano, adaptive difficulty, scores or leaderboards.

Avoid rearchitecting track generator if a focused cross-section width function and improved elevation profiles suffice. Simplicity and playable impact > abstraction.
