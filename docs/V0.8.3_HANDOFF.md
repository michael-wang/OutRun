## Controller

Standard mapping candidates: SHARE button 8 captures a local PNG; OPTIONS button 9 plays, pauses, or resumes with the one-second countdown; Touchpad click button 17 toggles fullscreen if an 18th button is exposed, otherwise R3 button 11. L1/R1 praise and L2 brake remain independent. Controls shows actual connected device ID, mapping, button count and last changed button index: use it to verify the physical DS4, because mapping differs by browser and connection. Unsupported nonstandard mapping is reported rather than guessed. Fullscreen may be denied by browser activation policy; use F or click Fullscreen when instructed. `share-30.png`, `share-60.png`, and `pad-17-diagnostic.png` in `docs/evidence/v0.8.3/` are simulated-controller browser evidence, not proof of physical DS4 behavior.
