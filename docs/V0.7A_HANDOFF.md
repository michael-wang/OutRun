## Implementation and validation

- Eight typed, seeded profiles derive geometry from the shared inverse Required Steering calibration. Each includes approach/challenge/relief; 350 m behind and 1000 m ahead protect framing. Previews finish explicitly; the original route still loops.
- Handling constants, 1.75× pace, camera, audio, wheels and occupants are unchanged. Footprint/corridor accepts candidate width; normal driving retains its original width/boundary.
- Identical seed/revision reproduces geometry, grade and props. Replay clears inputs, steering interpolation, motion and camera initialization. Switching candidates reloads the document to release the preceding scene.
- Reviews use `outrun-brick-reviews-v1`, keyed by ID/revision. Export schema 1 includes catalog/engine version and each ID/revision/seed, autoQA, humanVote and 240-character capped note. Invalid records/votes are rejected safely. No backend, telemetry or automatic approval.
- Evidence scripts use an external development-only four-detent driver. Normal/production play has no autopilot. Recordings are real wall-clock rendering, not unit simulation presented as gameplay.
