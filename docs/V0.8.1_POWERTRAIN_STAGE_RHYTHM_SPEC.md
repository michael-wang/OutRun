### P0-A. Real-feeling six-speed 0→300 km/h acceleration curve

**Research basis, not "shift = subtract 5km/h":**
- Engine RPM and shift moments are not wheel road speed. Real shifts manage engine/shaft torque, possibly producing a brief **torque hole** and a natural speed plateau or small drop depending on current drag/grade. Direct per-shift changes to only the displayed numeric speed or forcibly subtracting velocity are INVALID. Motor torque, transmission ratio and rolling drag create acceleration.
- Effective wheel force should follow the current gear ratio and RPM/engine torque curve, with plausible torque/traction limit in low gears, drag increasing ∝ speed², rolling resistance and the already implemented real grade force. A simple arcade calibration is enough; no 3A multi-clutch dynamic vehicle simulation.
- During a full-throttle 1→2, 2→3, ... 5→6, include an observable short real wheel-force reduction/engagement/recovery (tuned e.g. ~0.15–0.35s). Some gear shifts, especially at higher speed, should show a **small genuine drop in km/h** under zero/low tractive force plus drag; others may show a short speed plateau. Requirement is real continuity + measurable negative/near-zero net acceleration around the shift, not a magic fixed speed decrement. Preserve the user-accepted warm RPM engine sound and shift cues.
- Overall accelerating from standing to 300 should be **nonlinear and progressively more challenging**. Lower gears/earlier speed intervals have greater typical longitudinal acceleration; higher gears and increased aero drag have lower acceleration approaching actual top speed. A full throttle 0→300 graph should visibly curve/flatten rather than be a straight line. The motor does not need to be physically indistinguishable from a specific Ferrari.
- Preserve **attainable actual 300 km/h** on a sufficiently long flat test road, coherent numeric/needle, RPM/gear progression and accepted **1.75× world-distance multiplier**. No fake SFX-only shifts or secretly different physics in Stage vs free drive; preserve braking/coasting, true hill grade, offroad penalty, speed-sensitive lateral handling, overall warm engine and quieter v0.7D.2 tire sound.

**Measure BEFORE edit** on actual local code: full-throttle speed vs time at 30fps/60fps, `0→100`, `100→200`, `200→300` incremental times, all five shift start/end, old/new engine RPM, wheel torque multiplier, longitudinal acceleration, speed before/minimum/after each shift, road world progress. Also include uphill/coast runs. The previously reported v0.7C.1 0→300≈21.46s is HISTORICAL, not proof current v0.8 trace is still identical. Set first candidate arcade calibration approximately 0→300 within ~20–30sec but preserve pacing delight; no hard artificially flattened seconds-per-gear. User's real feel test decides final tuning.

**Implementation direction for Codex to inspect/adapt, not mandatory code template:**
`rpm = speed / tireRadius * gearRatio[gear] * finalDrive` (with idle/clutch state handling);
`driveForce = engineTorque(rpm, throttle) * gearRatio[gear] * finalDrive * efficiency / tireRadius` (then traction cap);
`a = (driveForce * shiftTorqueFactor - drag(v) - rolling - gradeForce - brakeForce)/mass`.
Do NOT reimplement all existing code if equivalent pieces already exist. During shift, dynamic `shiftTorqueFactor` reduction should lower drive force and produce net a<0 when drag dominates. Keep dt stability and plausible high-speed terminal drag; avoid gear hunting and sudden RPM/velocity teleport.

### P0-B. Correct unit of Stage composition: L2 challenge core + L3 transition

**Actual r1 defect (directly in archived Seed 1 JSON):**
- 8 `beats` across a 13,500-unit Stage. Each beat contains exactly **ONE C + ONE E + ONE W placement**. Stage is no longer one global C/E/W, but it STILL fails to reuse what made the HUMAN-KEEP L2P pilot compositions fun: multiple successive C profiles and staggered overlapping profiles *within the same micro-experience*.
- Explicit C placement windows occupy only **6,143/13,500 = 45.5%** of route distance. The other **54.5% contains no explicit C**, although elevation/width can vary.
- Gaps between successive explicit C windows measure about **732–930 world units**, often felt as long empty straights, plus an opening 623 and final 973. Several gaps are due to *inside-beat lead-in/out* PLUS mandatory inter-beat connectors. Removing only a fixed 150-unit join will not solve the roughly 800-unit between-corner gap.

**New content contract:**
- **L2 = compact CHALLENGE CORE / compelling driving phrase**, not a sealed road that obligatorily includes a long generic straight approach and exit. An L2 MAY intentionally include varied internal phases (like the accepted pilots: e.g. C03→C08→C04 with overlapping hill and narrowing) and must be capable of contributing multiple sequential curvature placements where relevant. Preserve essential sightlines/braking warning but do not make every L2 pack 300–500 units of uninteresting straight just to start.
- **L3 Stage = scenario and pacing director**: choose next L2 experience, how they connect, and when to add OPTIONAL `TransitionBuffer`s (straight, slight sweeper, scenic reveal, speed-recovery, brake-warning). Buffers are stage-level with authored/seeded intention. They can be near zero when back-to-back multi-curve is enjoyable; long straights require a meaningful purpose, not fixed filler. Most buffers SHOULD be clearly shorter than the r1 ~800-unit no-curvature intervals, subject to visible geometry/brake safety.
- **Stage is not simply L2 + exact same straight + L2**: pick varying short/no medium connectors based on dramatic rhythm, actual previous/next curvature and world phase, and ensure underlying 3D centerline/elevation/width/tangent remain continuous. A long panoramic straight is welcome as a deliberate recovery reward. Don't blindly require a fixed event count, angle, curve occupancy percent or universally impossible planner.
- **Use existing multi-placement Pilot mechanics** rather than re-creating a whole new Stage engine. Revise `StageBeat` / `StageComposer` interface so at least SOME L2 motifs in Stage are structurally multi-curvature with staged/overlapping C/E/W, NOT 8 separately generated independent one-C/E/W rows. Pilot A/B/C each have richer C sequences; treat them as references/motifs, not direct static concatenations.
- Keep Stage nominal duration ~100–150sec by increasing **genuinely interesting content**, not by inserting empty road to hit distance/time. Target actual distance-based route length adjusted for the revised accelerating six-gear car and player speed. On Stage 01 record distance/time at reference speed and a slower human/braking driver.
- Keep sane visual warning for genuine brake-required hard corner. Do not force every section to be full-throttle, and do not preemptively ban extreme difficulty based on an invented AAA feasibility model. Prevent actual illegal same-level roadway overlap/terrain collisions and obvious unplayable jumps.

**Before/after metrics at EACH of ≥5 seeds**: Stage active curvature windows/total proportion; total and longest gaps without a non-trivial curvature event; reason & duration of intentional scenic/acceleration buffers; number of genuine L2 cores, non-trivial C sequences per core and how E/W overlap; curve direction changes and player correction/brake rhythm; actual grade and segment widths. Aim for a meaningfully less empty Stage than old Seed 1, but never claim a 65%-turn fraction automatically means fun.

### P0-C. Roadway width is mostly four lanes (distance-based)

**Actual Seed 1:** 8 Width W04 placements; 4 of these are programmed `4→2` bottlenecks. This is too frequent and sacrifices future ability to add traffic, overtaking and roadside obstacles. Cannot infer ACTUAL narrow-distance ratio from these counts; sample final resolved road halfWidth(s) and laneLine(s).

**First tuning intent / not a permanent gameplay rule:**
- **FOUR lanes are DOMINANT:** initial distance-fraction goal roughly 55–70% of Stage at stable 4 marked lanes.
- **SIX or EIGHT lanes support high-speed openness:** initial fraction ~20–35%.
- **TWO or THREE lanes are rare purposeful moments:** initial fraction <=10–15%, not EVERY OTHER beat; concentrate near believable canyon bottle neck, mountain switchback, harbor access/roadworks in future. It is okay for a seed to contain no 2-lane sections; the distribution should be curated rather than forcibly even. Restrict *consecutive* blind narrow technical curves as needed structurally.
- Keep road centerline/edge, asphalt mesh, actual offroad and recoverable shoulder, guardrails, props, lane markings and car footprint in sync. No painted "8 lanes" where collision still detects 2.
- If the current generator doesn't support stable REAL 3-lane markings (vs the fractional lane equivalent inside a 4→2 taper), **do not fake it**. Support odd 3 lanes as an optional focused extension only if compatible with source without widening project; otherwise keep 2-lane rare and defer full 3-lane art/marking to later.
- Do not let world biome or L1 challenge automatically force 2 lanes. Preserve L3 preference that canyon more often gets steep grade, BUT that doesn't mean every canyon road must be narrow.
