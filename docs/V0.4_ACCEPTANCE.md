## Hard mobile/platform regressions

- Pixel 8 landscape fullscreen/orientation/PWA fallback still works in emulation; real device success is UNVERIFIED if not actually tested.
- Press and hold gas+steer for 15s without browser context menu/selection, pointer cancellation no sticky controls, swapped control layout persists.
- 1.75× should not become uncontrollable because the **input response is too abrupt**. Conversely, cutting gain alone must not make challenging bends impossible.
- At the outer shoulder boundary outward hold remains bounded, no orbit/spin or auto-reentry; inward steering quickly recovers.
- Do not use a speed-based camera FOV/dolly zoom, fake HUD velocity or scene-only scrolling.

## Human playtest priorities

1. At 1.75×, are short direction inputs **precise**, while hard corners remain manageable?
2. Do Space/Enter work comfortably with A/D on PC without menu conflicts?
3. Is the speed display prominent but visually clean?
4. Is drift clearly **intentional** and fun, with tire squeal at appropriate moments?
5. Does the 6–7 / 3–5 / 6–7 / 3–5 / 2–3 / 3–5 rhythm feel like exciting arcade racing?
