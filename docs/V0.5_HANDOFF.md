## P0 changes and preservation

- `src/body-pose.ts` maps actual matching bend load to smooth visual targets: about 6° at .25, 20° at .50, 40° at .75 and 57° at loaded full lock. Near-straight input stays calm. Deliberate overcorrection can add a short accent only on a substantially loaded curve. Speed gates the envelope; existing rapid visual easing and quiet mild-turn audio are retained.
- Authoritative s/x, acceleration/braking, steering filter and collision/shoulder rules remain unchanged. Pose does not feed navigation or penalties. Chassis roll is limited to about 3.15° to keep wheels visually grounded through larger yaw.
- Expanded the existing Medium left into a 6-reference-second sweeper and High right into a 5⅙-second sweeper. Added turn integrals cancel exactly, preserving the accepted global pressure gain **0.60945271556**, curvature/steering mapping and Extreme geometry. No hidden segment grip changes.
- Left sweep: radius about 415m, 77.25° total heading change, need -.50 at 230km/h (about -.509 at 234). Right sweep: radius about 276m, 96.57° total, need +.75 at 230 (about +.763 at 234). Both retain cosine ramps, long controllable plateaus and full-gas capability. Original procedural FAST SWEEP arrow signs mark their approaches.
- Extreme remains radius about 173m with +1.20 demand at 230km/h, controllable near 190km/h after actual braking. Low, S, breathing straights, original coast scenery and fixed FOV/dolly remain.
