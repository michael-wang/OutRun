## Revised design hypothesis, for human test

- **L2 content contract:** a compact engaging micro-challenge / mini driving phrase with actual internal successive and overlapped L1 profile placements where warranted. Its gameplay essence does NOT force always having long generic straight approach/recovery. Avoid blindly stripping all approaches: a braking corner needs readable entry, but it can be shaped and composed by Stage.
- **L3 Stage role:** chooses which L2 to stage, how to connect their end/start curves, and if/when to insert a brief *buffer* (straight, shallow bend, downhill vista, braking read-ahead). Generic straight is not mandatory between every L2, and a curve-to-curve seamless transition is allowed. Buffer duration comes from distance and experience, NOT a universal fixed 150+ meters PLUS other hidden lead-ins.
- **Road-width policy:** 4 lanes dominant; 6–8 secondary; 2 or truly marked 3 lanes scarce special moments. Provisional distance-share targets: 4 lanes ~60%, 6–8 ~30%, ≤3 ~10%, as observed from resolved track width, not count of W profile *instances*. These are tuning targets, not a universal absolute ban or AAA feasibility planner. Introduce **real 3-lane segment** only if the current mesh+markings can support it cleanly; do not fake it with fractional lane equivalent during a taper.
- **Check output AFTER changes**: compare curve event occupancy, longest neutral curvature gap, multi-placement count per L2 and actual lane-width distance shares against the old Seed 1. No arbitrary hard-coded L1 count or constant demanded 65% curve occupation makes a road fun; human will play several seeds again.
- **Stage length** may remain ~100–150s at normal pace by including genuinely engaging L2 content, NOT by re-padding with empty road until a target distance is reached.
