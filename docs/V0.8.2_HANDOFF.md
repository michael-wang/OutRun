## Physical speed and shift evidence

`grade-shift-traces.json` contains integrated 30/60 Hz runs at flat, +5% and −5% grade, both throttle and coast. At 60 Hz, the full-throttle flat run settles near 303 km/h, sustained uphill near 294 km/h, and sustained downhill near 327 km/h; the 360 km/h defensive limit is not the ordinary flat top speed. `five-shift-comparison.json` and `shift-speed-comparison.svg` compare the v0.8.1 measured run with v0.8.2. At 60 Hz, the change in actual wheel speed from pre-shift to the minimum during each shift was approximately −0.54, +0.13, +0.77, +1.69 and +2.63 km/h respectively; the negative first value means speed continued rising during that early shift. Speed is integrated from drive force, road resistance, grade and the temporary driveline drag; no fixed speed subtraction is used.
