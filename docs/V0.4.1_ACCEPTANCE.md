## Steering comparison protocol

Compare historical v0.3.2, v0.4 and new version if source artifacts are available. With a repeatable start on the same straight at ~180–230 indicated km/h and 1.75×:

1. Left 150ms → neutral 500ms; then mirror right. Note time to first yaw response, peak yaw rate, decay, heading/road-relative offset after 0.5s of neutral.
2. Left 900ms → neutral 300ms → right 500ms; mirror direction. Note response, reversal delay, lateral path and degree of recovery.
3. Deliberate hard bend and S-bend: driver's actions rather than autopilot. Does steering become effective early enough, and can driver exit without crossing onto opposite shoulder?
4. Steering held at shoulder boundary: still bounded with no full-circle spinning or automatic road reentry.
5. Release game key/touch on blur/pause: no latched motion.

Avoid claiming a precise "feels like arcade" test from a single state assertion; inspect the **actual motion video**. Numbers are diagnostic, the human reviewer makes the final feel decision.

## Track continuity protocol

- At accepted 1.75× with a fixed speed and varied controls, traverse **at least 120 simulated seconds** or long enough to cross every procedural/cyclic boundary more than once if feasible. Test high-speed loop wrap and a slow turn as separate trajectories.
- Log sampled asphalt surface at car and multiple lookahead distances; detect holes/no hits, invalid normals, warped corners, extreme discontinuities and missing forward draw/clip visibility.
- Examine browser frames around any reported break (not only start/end snapshots); check road vs background geometry and camera horizon.
- If unavailable in the 41s reference or automated traces, report the exact **unverified** part and a reproducible user capture mechanism; do not silently remove ROAD-01.

## HUD tests

- Test analog needle at parked, ~80, ~150 and ~230 km/h: the needle must move appropriately and numeric speed must reflect the same underlying value.
- Compare desktop (e.g., 1280×720) and Pixel 8-like landscape (e.g., 960×432), plus 844×390, both left/right thumb layouts. Controls must still receive pointer events.
- Speed gauge must not clip, hide turn lookahead or overlap the player car excessively. Keep contrast on sea/sky/road scenes and adapt to cutouts.
