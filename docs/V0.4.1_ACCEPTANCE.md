## Minimum repeatable handling experiment

Use an identical straight/road scene at accepted pace 1.75× and stated high indicated speed:

- Pixel strip: touch x = 50%, then x = 25%, hold for 150ms, release; mirrored at 75%. Confirm commanded steer and actual yaw respond; on release yaw stops accumulating rapidly without snapping vehicle back to road tangent.
- Without lifting, move the pointer from 20%→50%→80% in one continuous swipe. Command crosses 0 and turns in the new direction promptly.
- PC: press A 150ms→release 500ms, D 150ms→release 500ms. Then A 900ms→neutral→D 600ms, reverse also. Plot normalized virtual wheel position, yaw rate, heading, road-relative lateral distance and speed.
- Phone two-touch: hold GAS 15s simultaneously with steering finger sliding; change handedness and reload; confirm no browser callout or sticky controls.
- Take a meaningful left and right bend deliberately, then release near exit; old uncontrolled yaw tail cannot drag the car across the opposite shoulder. It is acceptable for an incorrectly chosen heading to continue straight off-road — **the game must not auto-correct it**.

No acceptance solely from game-state values: render/inspect **actual motion video** for at least one short correction and one demanding bend. Note physical Pixel 8 playtesting separately.

## Track diagnosis

At high pace loop through the available track and segment boundaries for ≥120s of simulated forward driving; verify ray hits or mesh continuity at and ahead of vehicle, including scenery/horizon to catch missing visible surface. Record screenshots around every suspect seam. If inability to reproduce persists, include exact instrumentation and reproduction steps for the human without claiming solved.

## Preserve regressions

- World pace default 1.75×, no speed-linked camera zoom, car ~20–25% phone viewport width.
- PC A/D, Space, Enter, proper keyboard focus; phone fullscreen/orientation/PWA; swapped thumb layouts; safe areas; long-press suppression.
- Bounded shoulder, offroad penalty, no auto-follow or lane-center snap, real brake, responsive sound/mute and pause.
- Existing build/unit/Playwright run with no fatal browser errors.
