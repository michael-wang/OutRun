## P0-A — Direct, stable, forgiving arcade steering

**Fixing v0.4 means investigating the implementation changes, not adjusting an arbitrary sensitivity multiplier.**

1. Diff `43fba35` / latest v0.3.2 versus `04a1f2b` / local v0.4 code. Trace digital control input → steering target/filter → vehicle yaw rate/heading → velocity/travel direction/side slip → lateral road position. Document the specific mechanisms causing delayed initial action and/or continued turn after release.
2. At accepted `1.75×` actual world pace, **steering must feel immediate**. A short digital tap should make a small but visible deliberate correction without crossing multiple lanes. A sustained press should turn strongly enough to navigate tighter bends. **No heavy input ramp that makes the first half-second ineffective.**
3. When direction input is released, **uncommanded yaw angular velocity and transient side-slip should decay quickly**, rather than continue steering the car through another lane. Similarly, pressing the opposite direction should respond promptly; do not require waiting through seconds of residual steering state.
4. Do not automatically center the car in its lane, rotate the car to road tangent, solve corners for the player, or introduce speed-dependent snap-to-road. Damping **residual turn dynamics** is not the same as correcting road position. If an arcade hybrid world/road-relative movement representation solves the problem better, use it without reintroducing v0.1's free curve following.
5. Both directions must be symmetric; PC digital keys and Pixel two-thumb buttons share the same handling logic. Avoid input cancellation sticking. Steering at rest should not teleport position; braking can help make hard corners controllable.
6. Preserve controlled intentional drift and tire squeal tied to actual significant slipping; gentle steering should remain quiet. **Don't add more slide/realistic yaw inertia** just to retain previous drift visuals. If necessary reduce physical inertia and use a limited **rendered body yaw effect** for dramatic but controllable drifting, with heading/travel relationship coherent.
7. Benchmarks from an identical straight at e.g. 180–230 indicated km/h, `pace=1.75`:
   - **Tap:** 120–200ms left and right presses: steering must begin within a couple rendered frames, produce a modest predictable offset and then STOP accumulating extra yaw soon after release.
   - **Hold:** 0.7–1.2s sustained press: enough authority to take an exciting corner at a sensible driving speed, with no immediate uncontrollable spin.
   - **Reverse:** left-hold → release → right-hold (and inverse): response reverses promptly, with no excessive inertia/oscillation; actual vehicle doesn't drift into the far shoulder after a completed corner solely because of stale steering state.
   - Demonstrate behavior in REAL browser driving, not merely set "steering" to a number in tests. Use recorded steering input, yaw rate, heading error, lateral position and speed traces. Compare v0.3.2, v0.4 and fixed version if possible.
8. Tune the physics/input together under a clear **arcade driving goal: press → respond; release → stabilize; countersteer → respond**. The owner tests subjective feel on Pixel 8.

## P0-C — Original analog speedometer LEFT of player car

The desired UI is an arcade **circular analog speedometer with a clearly animated needle**, plus bold digital speed, placed visually **left of the car**, not upper-right.

- Reframe existing DOM/CSS/SVG/Canvas HUD as appropriate; lightweight markup/procedural styling is better than a heavy new graphic package. The dial should have clear tick marks/scale (e.g. 0–260 km/h or coherent maximum), a radial sweep, colored upper-speed region, functional needle rotation from actual speed, and readable digital value + `km/h`.
- The speedometer should look visually important, with punchy/high-contrast arcade style and **custom original treatment** (not SEGA/Ferrari logos, exact dial art or copied icons).
- **Desktop:** sit low and to the left of the on-screen car, as in the supplied reference, but keep the road's approaching curve visible. Do not return to the v0.4 upper-right card.
- **Pixel 8 landscape:** same car-left visual relationship where possible but **must not overlap the bottom-left steering buttons or either handedness's thumb zones**. On tight viewports allow a smaller gauge slightly ABOVE the thumb-control row, still left of the car. Correctly respect safe-area/cutout and no pointer interception (`pointer-events: none` on noninteractive gauge).
- Dial + numeral must be readable during motion and when parked, no clipping/obscuring car, menus or essential road. Coordinate with existing fixed car framing ~20–25% screen width and fixed FOV/dolly. Gauge remains stationary in screen space (no annoying sway with car).
- Use actual game speed for numeric readout and needle; no fake increase from the 1.75× display multiplier. Needle movement should be smooth and responsive; verify at stopped/medium/high indicated values.
- Maintain compact Pause/Menu; don't add a huge new HUD.
