## Single question to answer

**Can we produce a reproducible ~5-minute Pacific Road Trip using a small catalog of human-approved gameplay bricks, where changing the seed yields a trip that is genuinely different AND still enjoyable?**

If the answer is NO, do not proceed to a Garage/economy.

## Technical reuse

Preserve from v0.6: Three.js / TypeScript / Vite browser build; Arcade Lateral Balance and inverse Required Steering→curvature, 1.75× world pace, 4-notch soft-detent steering, responsive drift/car/occupants and rolling wheels, three scenic acts, current camera, car-left speedometer, original locally bundled assets, Pixel 8 fullscreen/PWA/touch and PC mapping.

## What the owner should be able to PLAY

### 1. Brick Lab — intentionally tiny review UI

- A hidden/simple `?lab=bricks` route or menu for the **8 proposed bricks** from `docs/CHALLENGE_BRICK_CANDIDATES.md`, with Next Candidate / Replay Same / Keep / Rework / Reject and optional one-line note. Review accessible on PC and Pixel 8.
- Same candidate ID/revision/seed always recreates the same short challenge and contextual entry/exit.
- Local lightweight persistence for review choices; ability to export a small approval JSON/file, ideally with source-controlled approved revisions. Do not require user account or a database.
- AUTO_VERIFIED is NOT HUMAN_APPROVED. The generator must distinguish them, never put REJECTED/REWORK bricks into ordinary trips.

### 2. Pacific Road Trip / Intermediate / 5 Minutes — the first Guided Journey

- Exactly one initial guided preset; don't make a giant menu of fictional themes. Use current **Azure Coast → Golden Canyon → Sunlit Coastal City** with meaningful variation in segment order, micro-landmarks, direction, grade and tempo. Theme order may initially be guided/fixed, but the road/brick sequence must change with seed.
- The user's ~5 minutes is a **nominal duration** calibrated to a representative driving speed and the accepted 1.75× world pace; braking/new players take longer. Generate a target **travel distance**, not a five-minute timer that spawns bends.
- Use a deliberate experience arc: ~30–60s accessible launch, intensifying skill moments and vistas, a memorable climax, then relief/finish. Several 30–75s scenic acts are okay; avoid changing environments so frequently that transitions feel arbitrary. Reuse the three existing art packs, no new snow/volcano assets yet.
- UI: `Start Journey`, `Another Route` (new seed), `Replay This Route` (same seed) and visible compact `Journey Code` (seed + generator revision + preset/vehicle). No backend needed.
- Five-minute distance at ~234 displayed km/h and v0.6's ~1.75× world travel can be **tens of kilometers in world scale**. Use bounded road/scenery chunk streaming/recycling or proven geometry windows rather than building excessive full-journey meshes/objects up-front. Benchmark Pixel-like device resolution.
- Do not alter the accepted player driving model to make generated bricks pass; fix generator constraints or revise/discard individual bricks.

### 3. Mystery Drive — small early experiment or next milestone

A `Surprise Me` button uses the SAME generator and **approved** pool with a different JourneyIntent (allow unknown scene order and beat arrangement within art/comfort constraints). Only include in v0.7 if the Brick Lab and Pacific 5-minute guided route are sound. Otherwise make it the first scoped feature of v0.8.

- Never imply snow, volcano or a new physical hazard exists before those content packs/mechanics are built.
- Unknown route identity is fun; unknown unfair difficulty isn't. New seed reveals itself through the drive; replay code works the same way.

## Minimal source concepts, not a new framework

`JourneyIntent`: mode, nominalDuration, allowedSceneActs, challengeEnvelope, carRuleset, seed, generatorVersion.

`ChallengeBrick`: versioned/approval-marked gameplay segment profile, preconditions, entry/exit geometry and lane widths, required-steering vs reference speed, visibility, scenery compatibility, rarity/anti-combinations.

`BeatPlan`: ~6–10 significant emotional beats for a 5-minute trip, not a timer-driven mission script; each can use one or a few bricks and transitions.

`GeneratedJourney`: deterministic sequence of instantiated approved bricks with generated track/landmark geometry and metadata (distance anchors, expected safe line/speed, stage boundaries).

Avoid building a general visual editor or a huge AI planner to get this working.

## Generation acceptance and guardrails

### Static/deterministic checks
- The same `Journey Code` produces identical brick IDs/order, section curvatures/width/elevations, scene assignments and procedural prop placements at the same generator version.
- Track centerline, road geometry, shoulders, skyline/culling and scenery transitions remain continuous through long trips. No abrupt turn/grade jumps or objects blocking road/camera sightline.
- Every challenge honors its mechanic constraints: High/Extreme meaningful, braking requires visible warning and a practical lower-speed path, full-speed sweepers remain flat-out navigable where labelled.
- Consecutive bricks satisfy entry/exit and fatigue limits; no endless max load or several extreme blind crests.
- Generated seeds are evaluated with a deterministic scripted driver / no-steer comparison, **PLUS actual rendered browser drive**; on failure retry with a new valid candidate or report a rejected seed. Test should never quietly inject autopilot into normal gameplay.
- Avoid full-run memory/frame spikes in desktop & Pixel-like viewports. Physical Pixel 8 performance needs human validation.

### Concrete v0.7 evidence targets

1. **Brick review:** at least 8 candidate previews, human acceptance results for the ones actually reviewed; aim for >=5–6 approved before claiming a fun approved catalog. Do not fabricate ratings to satisfy automation.
2. **Guided mode:** at least one 5-minute Pacific Road Trip made from approved bricks, playable on PC/Pixel, with meaningful landscape and challenge progression.
3. **Replayability:** compare at least **10 deterministic candidate seeds** under the same preset. Check 100% can generate continuous drivable roads and that different seeds actually alter **challenge order/turn signs/grade and visible landmark distribution**, not only numerical IDs.
4. **Perceptual comparison:** provide at least 3 clearly different seed run videos/overviews, and let Michael answer *"Would I happily drive another one?"*. If repetitive, mark QUALITY FAIL even if code/test suites pass.
5. **Replay:** choose a saved seed and reproduce it exactly; the generated route and assets must stay deterministic at equal generator revision, without depending on nondeterministic `Math.random` calls.
6. **No gameplay regressions:** retained 1.75× handling, explicit brake-required vs full-speed corners, scene transitions, animation, analog steering/speedometer, Pixel 8 fullscreen/PWA/safe area and long-press behavior.
7. **No online service requirements:** local run and LAN/PWA deployment remain supported; no need for AI model calls or paid backend at game runtime.

## Decision gate and scope

**Human GO:** approved bricks create >=2 subjectively memorable distinct trips from a small sample, and the owner wants to restart without monetary rewards.

**NO-GO or revise:** different seeds feel like the same left-right road, challenge is unfair/unreadable, content becomes repetitive within five minutes, or phone performance/handling regresses.

**Defer:** RPG currency/garage/new cars, adaptive player model, forked roads, actual collision traffic/obstacles, snowy mountain/volcano art/mechanics, giant stage editor, online leaderboards, login and cloud saves.
