# Arcade HUD layout tuning

Layout-only candidate on `dev`; `main` is unchanged. Runtime changes are confined
to `src/style.css`. Gauge artwork, needle mapping, gameplay, input processing,
telemetry, streaming and performance logic are unchanged.

## CSS values

- Mobile gauge: `clamp(140px, 38dvh, 164px)` → `clamp(104px, 29dvh, 124px)`.
- Bottom offset: safe-area-aware bottom margin + 72 px → margin + 50 px.
- Center the gauge on the 144 px steering bar. Mirror it to the lower-right when
  controls are swapped, keeping the left-side pedals unobstructed.
- Steering bar height: 52 → 48 px; width and input range remain unchanged.
- Gauge/bar gap: 2 px. `pointer-events: none` remains unchanged.
- Mobile digital speed: 44 → 36 px; KM/H: 10 → 9 px; gear: 17 → 14 px.
- Hide the small mobile driving-status caption to keep it out of the steering
  area. Remove the prior upper-screen small-landscape positioning overrides.
- Desktop gauge: 200 → 160 px; digital speed: 54 → 44 px, keeping its bottom anchor.

At 915 × 412, the gauge changed from `(167.5, 159.5), 156.5 px` to
`(36.3, 218.5), 119.5 px`: about 59 px lower and 24% smaller. Visual review
confirmed that it stays below the scenic view and is no taller than the visible
car body in this shot. Automated checks also bound it by the projected car's
height and verify its alignment above the steering bar.

## Verification

- Production build and all 100 unit tests passed.
- 20 Edge browser tests passed: HUD layout, legacy keyboard/touch controls,
  swapped controls, digital speeds 0/100/266/320, needle accuracy, all six gears,
  control hit targets and mobile performance probe.
- Checked 915 × 412, 844 × 390, 960 × 432, 667 × 375, 568 × 320 and desktop
  1280 × 720, in normal and swapped layouts.
- No gauge overlap with Menu, center trip text, pedals, Camera or feedback
  buttons. Steering remains 144 × 48 px on mobile and supports simultaneous gas.
- Browser-emulated performance remains approximately 60 FPS with unchanged
  WebGL draw counts. [Performance data](evidence/v0.9-hud-layout/performance.json).
- These are desktop Edge browser-emulation results, not physical Pixel 8 acceptance.
