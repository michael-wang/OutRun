# v0.2 — Driving Is a Game

**Base:** Playable v0.1 on local `codex/first-drive` (or its successor). Reuse existing code.  
**Goal:** Make steering meaningful and establish **Pixel 8 landscape fullscreen** as a permanent platform requirement.  
**Order:** driving agency → off-road penalty → Pixel 8 fullscreen/PWA → camera/speed → hills → brighter art.

## P0.1 — Fix the core driving error
v0.1 tracks its road-relative lane position automatically, even around turns. That is **not** acceptable gameplay.

- Car yaw/heading and position must advance based on user steering, velocity and arcade dynamics, not silently rotate with road spline tangent every frame.
- Road geometry is used for height, boundaries, surface and progress but must **not steer** the car. No hidden lane keeping, center snap or automatic corner following.
- At a significant left or right bend, with sufficient speed and **zero steering**, the car must progressively miss the bend, leave asphalt and remain off-road until the user corrects. On the **same bend** with deliberate steering, the driver must be able to stay on the road.
- A world-space car pose/heading works. A Frenet/track-relative mathematical representation is allowed **only** if heading error correctly evolves against curvature, making hands-off turns fail. Do not force the world heading to the spline tangent.
- Keep gas/brake independent; steering at rest must not teleport/slide the vehicle laterally. Arcade response must be responsive and forgiving, not a rigid-body simulator.

## P0.2 — Off-road penalty and recoverable drift
- Detect vehicle footprint crossing an **asphalt edge**, not merely leaving a lane centerline.
- Off-road friction/drag/speed cap must be clearly noticeable. Guideline: under the same throttle and similar starting speed, speed becomes **25–40% lower within about 3s** than a comparable on-road drive. Tune by actual playtesting, do not make instant teleport or instant full stop.
- Road reentry releases the penalty and allows normal acceleration. Modest dust/rumble is welcome. An unrecoverable position can be restarted.
- Visually meaningful car yaw/slip, controllable correction and tire squeal tied to **actual slip**. Avoid drifting with only cosmetic body rotation while motion stays rail-locked.
- Add dev-only inspectable telemetry for speed, world heading / heading error, road curvature, lateral offset, asphalt-edge / off-road state and drift slip.

## P0.3 — Pixel 8, landscape fullscreen, installable PWA
**Target:** Google Pixel 8 running Android Chrome. Display is 20:9 at 2400×1080 physical landscape resolution; **CSS viewport and DPR are not fixed to those physical pixels**.

### Route A: Chrome tab
- Mobile **Drive / Fullscreen** tap (a real user gesture) must attempt `gameRoot.requestFullscreen()`; when permitted, call `screen.orientation.lock("landscape")`. Detect success, errors and unsupported APIs. The phone may refuse; never freeze, trap the player or pretend fullscreen succeeded.
- Offer a visible Fullscreen retry/exit/pause path and a friendly Rotate Device overlay when portrait or orientation lock fails.
- True fullscreen is not equivalent to CSS `position:fixed`. Handle `fullscreenchange`, `resize`, `orientationchange`, blur/visibility and input cancellation without stuck throttle.

### Route B: installed PWA
- Add a valid manifest for an **original neutral prototype name** (not the SEGA brand), `display: "fullscreen"`, `orientation: "landscape"`, correct scope/start URL, bundled valid **192×192 and 512×512 PNG** icons.
- Ensure it is installable on a qualifying Chrome Android origin; add only minimal required PWA wiring. Android installed PWA fullscreen is a different mode from the Fullscreen API: do not require `document.fullscreenElement` to be non-null to recognize PWA display mode.
- No hosted/public deployment required. If installability or orientation cannot be checked on physical Pixel 8, mark **UNVERIFIED**.

### Mobile UI and speed
- Preserve two thumbs: **left** left/right steering, **right** vertical accelerator above brake; Settings swaps both groups and persists after restart. Touch areas ≥56 CSS px, comfortably separated with visible press states.
- Use viewport meta with `viewport-fit=cover`, responsive canvas, dynamic viewport/safe-area handling and letterboxing **only if necessary**. No scrolling, accidental zoom, cutout or system-gesture overlap in landscape. When orientation changes, resize renderer/camera correctly.
- Concurrent steering+gas, steering+brake, opposite release order, cancellation and focus loss work; never keep controls stuck.
- Target **smooth ≥30 FPS sustained, preferably near 60**, on real Pixel 8. Use modest pixel ratio/shadows and optimize scene rather than hardcoding resolution. Only claim measured FPS when hardware was actually used.

## P1 — Camera and speed perception
- **No speed-driven FOV changes and no speed-driven camera dolly/zoom.** Fix a sensible chase FOV/distance/height; gentle heading smoothing and terrain following are okay.
- Camera follows the **car** rather than stabilizing around road centerline. Allow recognizable drift angle and adequate look-ahead.
- Car in v0.1 is visually too small. Increase its on-screen presence without obscuring bends. Verify both desktop and phone screenshots.
- Sense of speed should come from road markings, nearby props passing quickly, scenery parallax and smooth camera response, not zoom or excessive shake.

## P1 — Stronger, visible road elevation
- Retain the continuous 3D coastal road, both bend directions.
- Add a clear **climb → crest → descent**, visible from chase camera, with meaningful horizon change and temporarily hidden road beyond the crest. Smooth contact, normals and mesh joins; avoid floating wheels and jolting terrain.
- Mix readable straights, sweeping turns and a stronger corner for driving rhythm.

## P2 — More energetic coastal art
- Preserve sunny Mediterranean scene, but make it more vivid and exciting: stronger light/dark contrast, readable asphalt and markings, richer sea blues, better vegetation/material distinction and roadside speed markers.
- Don't blanket-increase saturation or pivot to a neon night theme. No wholesale asset rewrite before driving fundamentals are correct.
- Optional minor UI refinements only after above priorities.

## Scope and deliverables
- Preserve current v0.1 Three.js/TS/Vite app and tests; extend them to detect the old bug. No competitors, garage, levels, scoreboards, new physics engine or public deployment.
- Dev-only deterministic state/test hooks are acceptable, but no cheat input should run during real play.
- Run build/unit/Playwright and actual browser visual review; use `docs/V0.2_ACCEPTANCE.md` and `docs/PIXEL8_PLAYTEST.md`.
- Create `docs/V0.2_HANDOFF.md` showing exact PASS/FAIL/UNVERIFIED results. Stop for human playtest at v0.2.
