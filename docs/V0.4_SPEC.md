# v0.4 — Arcade Handling & Rhythm

**Product milestone:** Make the accepted **1.75×** pace **controllable and exciting**, put speed front-and-center, then add purposeful drifting and arcade road rhythm.  
**Base:** Existing playable local v0.3.2 (`43fba35` implementation, `cd36798` handoff).  
**Owner feedback:** 1.75× speed feels good enough to proceed, **but left/right controls have an overly strong response**; PC keys should be `A/D` steer, `Space` accelerator, `Enter` brake; displayed speed is buried low-left.

## Priority and scope

- **P0 must work:** predictable high-speed digital steering, exact desktop keys, conspicuous speed HUD, accepted world pace preserved.
- **P1 should work after P0:** controllable exaggerated drift with sensible squeal trigger; denser, intentionally timed road sequences.
- **NOT in v0.4:** new convertible model, driver/passenger animations, scenic themes, rivals or races. These are real product requests in `docs/ARCADE_DESIGN_BACKLOG.md` and will be tackled after the combined driving/track system is approved.

## P0-A — Steering feel: tone down twitchiness, not driving speed

**Important:** The user accepts the new visual speed but finds the steering input overly aggressive. Do **not** revert the world-pace multiplier to solve it.

1. Keep `highSpeedPace = 1.75` as default everywhere the normal game starts (desktop, Pixel 8 fullscreen, PWA). Keep dev A/B `?pace=...` tests where useful; the UI need not expose four pace choices.
2. Inspect the existing digital steer event pipeline and speed-dependent yaw/turn rates. Identify if v0.3.2 overcompensated steering gain when longitudinal pace rose. Measure response in terms of **heading change and road-relative lateral displacement**, not simply by whether the vehicle is still on-screen.
3. Implement intentional progressive digital steering: a short A/D or thumb-button press is a **small precise line correction**, and a sustained hold progressively develops enough turn rate for tight corners. Use controlled ramp-in/ramp-out and speed-aware steering authority where helpful; choose measured tunings after playtesting, not magic numbers alone.
4. Right/left must be symmetric; buttons must not stick on blur/cancel; holding both opposite steering directions must have deterministic safe behavior. Steering at a stop must not shift vehicle sideways. Don't create invisible lane-keeping, forced recentering or curvature-following.
5. Preserve the user's ability to **make meaningful high-speed turns with deliberate sustained input and/or braking**. Do not make the car understeer so much that the road is impossible. Keep off-road mistakes and penalties meaningful, bounded shoulders and inward-only rescue working.

**Indicative calibration test:** On a standardized straight starting at indicated ~234 km/h and 1.75×, a 150–200 ms steer tap should result in a modest correction (not traverse a full lane). A sustained 700–1200 ms hold should produce a clear turn sufficient for the authored exciting bend at a reasonable chosen speed. Tune the exact lateral and yaw trace with real browser recordings and reference road widths; report quantitative before/after values rather than asserting that it "feels smoother". **The goal is precision with skill headroom**, not effortless driving.

## P0-B — Desktop keyboard mapping

In active desktop gameplay:

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `A` | Steer left |
| `D` | Steer right |
| `Space` (held) | Accelerate |
| `Enter` (held) | Brake |

- Use `event.code` where practical: `KeyA`, `KeyD`, `Space`, `Enter`. Handle `keydown`/`keyup` and keyboard repeat correctly so held input stays active.
- Prevent browser Space scrolling and **Enter activating a focused menu button** during gameplay. Do this with appropriate event/default management and focus control; do **not** disable Enter's accessibility activation of ordinary buttons while paused, in menu, in settings or when typing.
- Update in-game instructions, relevant README/help, and tests. Old W/S/arrow mappings may remain only if they do not interfere or confuse; **primary displayed/verified controls must be exactly A/D, Space, Enter**.
- Simultaneous Space+A/D and Enter+A/D must work; gas+brake concurrency has defined deterministic precedence; release and window blur cannot latch input.
- Pixel 8 touch steering left/right, stacked gas/brake, swapped hand layout and long-press protection remain unchanged.

## P0-C — Make the speedometer a key visual element

Speed is a primary reward, currently tucked away too inconspicuously near the lower-left corner.

- Promote speed to a **large, distinctive arcade-style display** with dominant three-digit value and clearly legible km/h label, good contrast and typography, optional modest dial/speed bar. Visually communicate acceleration without resorting to fake speed metrics.
- **Preferred starting position: upper-right corner** on desktop and landscape Pixel 8, with responsive safe-area margins, away from the forward road focus and thumb buttons. Codex can adjust if real screenshot comparison reveals overlap or clutter.
- Use meaningful scale (starting design: value roughly 40–60 CSS px on phone landscape, responsive on desktop), but do not obscure near bends, road horizon, settings overlay or camera view.
- Keep Menu/Pause compact. Make speed visible while driving, including on Pixel 8 20:9 / 960×432-ish CSS viewport. Use actual camera/game speed reading, not independently animated counterfeit speed.
- Check both default and swapped touch layouts, safe-area cutouts, fullscreen/orientation change and legibility at rest and 234-style high speed.

## P1-A — Intentionally triggered, expressive arcade drift

User observed frequent tire squeals from gentle steering and a car that cannot visibly yaw far enough in turns. We're building an exciting **arcade racer**, not realism.

- Separate **heading**, motion/travel direction, and **rendered body slip/yaw** as needed. Prefer controllable arcadey dynamics rather than cosmetic angle only. Drift must alter how car enters/exits a curve enough to feel interactive while retaining road/shoulder constraints and recoverability.
- Design a clear gentle turn vs deliberate hard corner distinction. **Gentle steering should not start tire squeal**. Detect meaningful slip (speed + yaw/slip angle/lateral acceleration as appropriate) using hysteresis/minimum persistence so squeal doesn't chatter on/off.
- During clear high-speed drift, the car should show recognizable sideways attitude, with an indicative visual slip angle **up to ~20–40°** in well-executed strong turns (tune for fun/camera/road readability, no mandatory constant 40°).
- Steering reversal/counter-steer and brake/throttle should provide opportunities to stabilize or deepen the slide; no full-circle spinning, endless uncorrectable drifting or automatic track centering.
- Recheck fixed chase camera so the slide is visible and road ahead remains navigable. Existing audio mute/gesture unlock, phone performance and no speed zoom must work.
- Test gentle steering with no/rare squeal and strong-sustained steering with proper squeal at high speed; validate real rendered yaw, not just slip flags.

## P1-B — Designed arcade road rhythm

Increase **curvature challenge per unit of road distance** rather than making steering more sensitive. The desired cadence is a designer's beat map, **measured at representative 1.75× fast travel**:

| Segment | Target feel duration | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 6–7s | Open acceleration / readable line |
| B | 3–5s | Exciting but readable corner |
| C | 6–7s | Relative ease / regain speed |
| D | 3–5s | Second stimulating bend / S transition |
| E | 2–3s | Very dangerous short bend requiring skill or braking |
| F | 3–5s | Recovery stretch / enjoyment |
| Repeat with variation | | No monotonous constant maximum curvature |

- Author segment lengths and curvature relative to **track distance**, not literal real-time triggers (slower players stay longer in corners).
- Keep gentle transitions/crest sightlines where needed; avoid abrupt spline kinks, clipping, blind impossible turns, excessive scenery or severe roads that can't be driven at any reasonable speed.
- The intended challenge: expert line/setup/braking preserves more average speed than holding GAS and randomly steering. Test at least one representative rhythm cycle using actual play/automated driver.
- The new less-twitchy controls and authored corners must be jointly tuned. A road can be challenging **because of its geometry**, not because controls are frustrating.

## Regressions and delivery

Preserve actual 1.75× speed/world travel, responsive acceleration/brake, v0.2.1 off-road corridor and drag, no free curve-follow, fixed camera FOV/dolly, mobile fullscreen/PWA/multitouch/handedness/long-press prevention, original car and sound. Do not change visual vehicle size just to hide handling issues.

Implement locally in existing Vite/Three.js game, run actual app/build/unit/Playwright, inspect at least a **straight short-tap vs strong bend/drift video** plus desktop/mobile screenshots. Handoff measured steering response, speed/handling preservation, audio-state evidence, actual curves and known issues. **Stop at v0.4 for human review.**
