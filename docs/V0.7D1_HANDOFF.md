Starting at 230 km/h, gas ended at 282.4 / 267.6 / 252.9 and coasting at 192.1 / 175.3 / 158.4. `grade-unit.json` contains the traces; `grade-browser.json` samples actual pilot ascent and descent. A stopped car remains stopped without input.
- **GRADE-03 PASS for automated checks:** `grade-fps-pace.json` compares 30/60 Hz integration and 1×/1.75× progress; the force is based on the real road tangent, without multiplying by pace. Rendered pilot recordings cross crests and grade changes.
- **SLIP-01/02 PASS in automated driving:** at the same bend and steering for 0.5 s, fixed-speed unit lateral displacement at 150 / 230 / 300 km/h was -0.27 / -1.65 / -3.55 m; required steer was 0.286 / 0.500 / 0.743. `speed-turn-unit.json` and `speed-curve-browser.json` include the controlled comparisons. The 230 km/h calibration is unchanged. The unchanged corridor constraint and full browser regression cover both sides.
- **SQUEAL-01/02 PASS for signal and matched simulation; physical listening UNVERIFIED:** previously the load term required steering and curve demand to have the same sign, so countersteering during genuine lateral motion could be quiet; the sound envelope itself had a 0.10 s onset and 0.18 s release, with no direction-specific cooldown. Mirrored tests, gentle-quiet and pause tests pass. `audio-capture.json` and `audio-L2P-*.webm` contain real mixed browser-audio captures from both signs and multiple pilots plus a gentle turn. `src/sound.ts` and the warm engine graph are unchanged. Focused Enter/Space, in-game Enter brake, P/R/N/B/C/M/F/E, K/W/X, 1–5, settings Escape, IME/text isolation and gamepad focus navigation were tested. Keyboard screenshots are included.
