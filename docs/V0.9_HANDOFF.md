Each finished at about 69,000 route units and without page errors. Seed 2 predates separate telemetry storage; seed 3 predates the development-only `stream=0` guard. All three recordings predate the final review-reader and connector-anchor metadata fixes. Those changes do not change the default rendering route or driving physics. Every one-second sample had the current and next two road chunks ready; shorter gaps cannot be excluded by this sampling. Full recordings and telemetry: `docs/evidence/v0.9/trip-seed-{1,2,3}-full.webm` and corresponding `trip-seed-{1,2,3}-report.json`. The preliminary pre-scenery seed-1 report is kept separately. Contact sheets sampled every 30 seconds (`trip-seed-{1,2,3}-contact.png`) were inspected in addition to transition images. `performance-summary.json` reports each biome boundary's nearest one-second sample and a four-second frame window: P95/P99 at all 15 windows were 16.8 ms, and the largest frame was 17.0 ms. These are observed desktop results, not a blanket guarantee or physical-device benchmark.

Seed 1 recorded all 70 chunks: maximum worker road preparation 210.3 ms and scenery preparation 705.5 ms, maximum single main-thread commit 0.3 ms, CPU-side upload submission 0.1 ms and disposal 0.2 ms. Preparation is off the render thread. An independent GPU timer audit recorded 7,205 buffer calls across all 70 chunks, no disjoint results and maximum individual query 1.123 ms; per-chunk sums and the limitations of driver-deferred work are in `gpu-upload-query.json`.

Controlled versus no-brake technical-corner comparisons on seeds 1 and 2: no-brake went off-road for 0.75 / 2.70 s; controlled braking stayed on-road. The route's actual four-lane and curve shares are sampled from geometry rather than inferred from placement counts. Detailed comparisons are in `brake-comparison.json` and `five-plans.json`.

## Human playtest and limitations

Please play at least two different Trip Codes, use Replay Same Route and New Route, leave praise or issue markers at interesting moments, and save a final KEEP / REWORK / REJECT and difficulty. Export feedback and the drive report from the review menu. Human enjoyment, pacing, perceived scenery continuity, and the desire to replay are not established by scripted runs.

**UNVERIFIED:** physical Pixel 8, physical PS4 controller, actual phone-to-PC LAN connectivity/firewall, and sustained device performance. The LAN URL returned HTTP 200 from this PC only. Sampled screenshots, contact sheets and one-second ready-window checks cannot exclude every short visual or timing transient. The known v0.8.4 hollow-looking distant road and sparse, regular palms remain; no v0.9.1 art pass was attempted. Worker scenery preparation reached 705.5 ms in the measured run, although it was off the render thread and road remained ready in the recorded samples. Initial readiness is longer than the prior short-stage benchmark; do not interpret the v0.8.5 two-minute numbers as a like-for-like long-route baseline.
