## Delivered changes

- Rolling wheels integrate actual world-distance delta / 0.46m radius. Front wheels have independent bounded ±0.3-radian steering pivots. v0.5 wheels were merged into the static body, causing the frozen-wheel defect. At high RPM, a band-limited blurred-spoke highlight avoids frame aliasing; the underlying axle still integrates physical travel. This is an intentional visual approximation, not a reduction of world speed. Stop/reset/pause cease rotation.
- One continuous 13,416.67m scenic loop, authored in the existing inverse-demand system. Solved neutral spans close the integrated route; no hidden positional correction or scene-dependent grip. Curvature calibration remains 0.6094527155598083 pressure gain. `src/handling.ts`, pace/input/camera behavior remain unchanged.
- Distance-based 600m overlaps introduce mixed props and blended terrain/sky/fog across all three boundaries, including City→Coast. Local deterministic geometry is built once and spatially instanced. No streaming screens or external assets.
- Both travelers have small torso/head/arm responses. Three two-part passenger hair locks hang at rest, stream back at speed and flutter asymmetrically through turns. Straight-line wind continues. Six cheap hair meshes and small pivots replace any need for cloth or a skeleton. Presentation does not feed back into physics.
