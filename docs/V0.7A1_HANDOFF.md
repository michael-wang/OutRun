## What changed and why

- `src/road-profile.ts` defines 3.5-unit lanes and smooth cosine width tapers. `brickWidth(s)` supplies one cross-section to asphalt vertices, edge paint, lane separators, shoulders, guardrails, posts, scenery anchors, car ground placement and handling bounds. Actual total paved widths are 7 / 14 / 21 / 28 units.
- Width changes lateral room only. Curvature profiles, seeds, durations, inverse RequiredSteer calibration, acceleration/braking forces, four soft detents, 1.75× pace and fixed speed-independent chase camera remain unchanged. No gravity speed boost or steering assist was added. Boundary contact is released when widening creates room; shrinking limits apply only at the outer corridor, never to cars already inside it.
- BRK-03 has 160 units of height range versus 60 in r1; peak climb is about 25%, steepest descent about 20%. These exceed the suggested starting grade range because the fixed chase view remained readable and grounded. The crest retains a visible advance warning and a moderate, non-braking descent bend.
- BRK-05 drops 198 units versus 98 in r1, with a sustained descent reaching about 16.9%. The same throttle model accelerates from 170 toward 234 km/h. The camera is unchanged; stronger perspective comes from actual road/terrain height.
- Lane lines terminate/merge near the moving outer edge; no median collision was added. Narrowings have advance signs and approximately 4–5 reference seconds of taper. Guardrails follow road height; canyon wall segments align with slope and lab building foundations extend into steep ground. Visual QA found exposed flat mesa bottoms during the steep descent; lab mesa skirts now extend below sea level, and coastal cliff bases extend farther into slopes. The visible tops and normal free-drive art remain unchanged.
- Normal free-drive retains its original road-width/marking design and accepted handling. No new bricks, game engine, external assets or dependencies.
