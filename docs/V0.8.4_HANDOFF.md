## Grounding and scenery

Each frame now measures four tire-cylinder low points against actual rendered asphalt or shoulder triangles. Visual-only wheel vertical offsets and small road-conforming contact shadows do not alter driving physics. `wheel-five-seeds.json` records four labeled contacts for five unchanged Stage seeds over uphill, downhill, crest, trough, 150/300 km/h left/right turns and shoulder cases (45 cases, 180 tire patches, maximum absolute measured residual below 0.001 mm in that sample); `wheel-steep.json` documents a steep test. The owner's original photos were not available as local files; these measurements do not prove the visual result is accepted by the owner.

Chunked low-poly support faces follow the road edges below the shoulder, and deterministic instanced city backdrop masses add depth. `scene-five-seeds.json`, `scene-seed-*.png`, `wheel-seed-*.png`, `pad-*-diagnostic.png` and `lan-photo.png` are browser evidence. The support audit measures finite vertices, chunk seams and alignment of support-top vertices with the shoulder. `scene-seed-1-before.png` and `scene-seed-1-after.png` show the same reported 8,426-unit viewpoint; `wheel-steep-before.png` and `wheel-steep.png` show the same steep side view. The v0.8.3 fingerprints for seeds 1, 2, 3, 42 and 710 remain respectively `828d4e5f`, `d9ebd51a`, `e95cb1b5`, `7a921419` and `7ed957de`. No difficulty or biome recipe changed.

## Performance and validation

A same-seed 15-second Edge comparison at emulated 844×390 measured approximately 60 fps in both versions; the sampled render used 138 draw calls / 84 geometries before and 150 / 90 after (`mobile-performance.json`). The first full regression run found two keyboard-driving failures during simultaneous recordings; the initial isolated rerun still failed one. Reducing redundant scene updates and raycasts made both cases pass in the isolated rerun while the four-wheel checks remained green. The subsequent isolated full rerun passed all 123 Playwright tests. JavaScript heap readings are coarse and do not measure GPU memory. A short 960×540 Edge run measured about 60 fps; the first full Seed 1 v0.8.4 recording was approximately 59.9 fps versus approximately 59.9 fps for the earlier v0.8.3 recording on the same desktop viewport. This is an observational comparison, not a physical Pixel GPU benchmark. The full regression and three-run results are recorded below.

To reproduce the archive audit, start a separate local dev server with `npm run dev -- --host 127.0.0.1 --port 5185 --strictPort`, then run `node scripts/v084-capture-audit.mjs`. It creates twelve additional files; it does not delete existing captures.

Three complete rendered, scripted drives are in `seed-1-braking-full.webm`, `seed-2-braking-full.webm` and `seed-3-braking-full.webm`, each with matching telemetry and finish image. They finished in 119.7, 118.6 and 119.4 seconds, with zero off-road time and no browser errors. These runs verify execution and provide visual material; they are not human acceptance.
