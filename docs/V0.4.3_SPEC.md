# v0.4.3 — Soft Steering & Honest Curves

**Base:** EXISTING playable local v0.4.2 (`d44c8e5` code, `f7ed029` handoff; inspect latest local HEAD).

**Owner verdict:** Gameplay-First / Arcade Lateral Balance direction WORKS. Preserve and refine, don't start over.

Human review observations:
- Steer input's **four notches are too visibly abrupt** in actual driving. Ambiguity: this may also refer to bluntly separated difficulty sections; fix both without losing calibration.
- Original OutRun 2 image shows dramatically larger car body rotation during a hard corner than current game, while the *most intense* original-game drifts can be even larger.
- **Gentle bends/corrections need little or no visible vehicle yaw**; strong bends should be unmistakably exciting.
- The prototype sometimes requires slowing down for a bend that **looks too mild**; the game needs visual/gameplay consistency.

This is an integrated calibration task. Do not add more game systems or expand scenic content.

## P0-A — Soft Detents: stable notches, fluid actuation

Keep the underlying v0.4.2 **9 stable effective targets**:
`{-1, -0.75, -0.5, -0.25, 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1}` with adjustable step count N=4 (not mandatory permanent value).

- Raw phone `uRaw` [-1,1] remains a continuous absolute-position horizontal steering strip. PC A/D move same virtual wheel. The chosen target `uQuantized` stays at an available level.
- Preserve tuned dead zone around center and **hysteresis** at boundaries, so 1–2 CSS px thumb vibration at a threshold does not produce repeated adjacent-switch events. Make threshold crossings responsive rather than sticky.
- Add a separate **short-lived output transition** `uApplied` toward `uQuantized`; first candidate e.g. 60–120ms notch-to-notch under held input, **faster return toward zero (~40–90ms)**. These are starting values for tuning, not fixed promises. Use delta-time-stable interpolation or short step response, NOT a long 0.5–1s inertia ramp.
- Critical: during constant held notch the effective correction is stable; at release the residual lateral speed cannot carry the car to the far shoulder. The steering-output interpolation is a control response filter, **not** an independent persistent physical velocity. Straight+neutral has dx/dt≈0.
- Show the touch marker visually continuous. An optional tiny dev-only indicator can show effective notch/applied value and help compare before/after. Do not lie about effective value.
- PC and Pixel 8 both use this same control stream. Swapped thumb layouts, two pointers, pause/blur/cancel, long-press prevention, fullscreen all remain.
- Compare v0.4.2 hard steps with v0.4.3 filtered steps on the **same bend/starting speed**, recorded as raw/target/applied/road-offset. Test both short tap and sustained correction. Player still needs to steer through the bend; no lane-centering helper.

Alternative soft-detent interpolation **across narrow transition bands** may be considered if it is noticeably smoother and retains wide stable detent plateaus. Do not expose a completely analog microprecision requirement again without explicit proof it is superior.

## P0-B — Make visually difficult curves correspond to difficult driving

**Core issue:** target `requiredSteer` (e.g. Extreme 1.2 at 230 km/h) can currently be achieved with curvature that LOOKS trivial, because the mapping `curvePressure(curvature, speed) / steeringAuthority(speed)` was tuned for numeric requirements, not visible road geometry.

Build one understandable **global calibration** between:
- RequiredSteer at reference speed;
- resulting road curvature/radius (generated inversely from desired demand);
- integrated heading change over segment distance (∫κ ds);
- camera-visible predicted bend over next ~1–3 seconds at accepted world pace;
- available road width, length and time to react.

**Chosen strategy:** Lower/retune curve-pressure gain relative to physical curvature where necessary, then regenerate stronger actual 3D bends for the same required steering targets. Maintain a unified mapping per vehicle/ruleset and do NOT add invisible per-section penalties or unattached fake visuals.

The inverse generator must still take a small challenge definition (`targetRequiredSteering, referenceSpeed, duration/length, width, elevation, direction`) and derive curvature and mesh. If one global tuning cannot yield legible + playable range, report the trade-off clearly and adjust the shared control/pressure function; do not silently label a curve Extreme with little visible bend.

**Checks:**
- At reference speed, keep approximately Low 0.25 / Medium 0.50 / High 0.75 / Extreme >1.0 required steering, plus signed S.
- **Monotonic visible severity:** Low should look easy, Medium show notable sweep, High look challenging, Extreme look alarmingly tight/readable in advance. Capture same-camera comparable bends and report numeric projected curvature/lookahead or integrated heading angle, not only a spreadsheet of requiredSteer.
- Extreme must still be **full lock insufficient at reference speed but braking/coasting early allows a complete on-road run**. Visible corner must warn the player early enough (no deceptive blind surprise) and remain drivable at the documented safe speed.
- Keep geometry/renderer/collision/road-edge/outward pressure consistent, and keep road-relative no-steer failure. At challenge joins, no discontinuity in heading or sudden pressure impulse unrelated to geometry.
- Use smooth curvature/elevation transition ramps (continuous derivative as practical) rather than abruptly switching from 0.25 to 0.75 demand. Core segment plateau remains testable. Duration target is based on **distance**, not wall-clock script.
- Maintain ≥120 seconds simulated high-speed road continuity and rendered samples over all seams/crest joins. Avoid building huge new roads or scenery.

## P1 — Exaggerated but conditional visual vehicle yaw

**User reference:** OutRun 2 sports car at a forceful corner, visibly sideways with strong body attitude. It is not a demand to copy Ferrari or SEGA artwork.

- Separate road-relative player movement (authoritative lateral balance) from **rendered sports-car body pose** and skid FX. Rendered yaw/lean must not change x, s, collision, camera road tracking or introduce physical yaw inertia.
- Mild steady correction (e.g. |uApplied|≤0.25, low imbalance/curve pressure): **little or no visible body yaw**, no frequent screech, no gratuitous smoke.
- Mid steer (~0.5): some visible pose where the curve demands it; avoid a fixed mechanical step.
- Strong input (~0.75–1.0) **and** substantial curve/side load or mismatch: nonlinear transition to dramatic body yaw (initial visual target about **25–40°**, allow up to **~45°** for strongest intentionally drifting pose). Tune by watching gameplay at speed and in camera, not just setting a rotation angle in code.
- Avoid yaw instantly snapping when crossing notches; use visual easing with quick recovery at exit, independent of physical position. Sign should correctly depict the corner/drift direction; crossing an S should smoothly reverse pose.
- Brake/throttle changes and speed may affect the expressive animation. Use meaningful threshold + hysteresis for tire squeal, with realistic mute/user-gesture constraints. This is stylized arcade art direction, not a tire simulator.
- A large cinematic car angle with perfectly centered steady path is acceptable as a stylized arcade pose ONLY when synchronized to actual demanding corner behavior; don't show 45° on an easy straight or imply invisible loss of control.
- Keep the chase camera FOV/distance speed-INVARIANT and the car/road framing stable.
