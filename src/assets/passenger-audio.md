The handclap is a single hit excerpted from [Solo Clap by OwlStorm](https://freesound.org/people/OwlStorm/sounds/151219/), released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The excerpt is trimmed, filtered and mixed to mono for the open cockpit.

The one-handed wave uses an offline Microsoft Zira recording of “Oh, yeah!” Both syllables are in one clip triggered with the gesture. “Oh” starts about 0.08 s into the lift, “yeah” starts at 0.47 s as the hand reaches the top, and the vowel trails off around 1.35 s during the descent.
