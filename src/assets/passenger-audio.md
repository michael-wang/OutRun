The handclap is a single hit excerpted from [Solo Clap by OwlStorm](https://freesound.org/people/OwlStorm/sounds/151219/), released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The excerpt is trimmed, filtered and mixed to mono for the open cockpit.

The one-handed wave uses a short “Yes!” synthesized offline with Microsoft Zira. The clip starts with the gesture. Its voiced accent lands about 0.48 s in, when her hand reaches the top, and the final consonant clears before she lowers it.
